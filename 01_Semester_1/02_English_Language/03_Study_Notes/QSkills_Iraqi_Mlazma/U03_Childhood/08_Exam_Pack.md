## Section 3 — Reading (10 × 1)

### اقرأ القطعة ثم أجب

> **The Kite**
>
> When I was ten years old, my father and I built a kite. We **had bought** the paper and the wood the week before, and I **had waited** for a windy day. Finally, on a Friday morning, we took the kite to the hill behind our house.
>
> My father held the string, and I ran with the kite. At first, it fell again and again. I wanted to stop. I felt **resentful** because I **had worked** so hard. My father did not laugh. He said, “A kite needs the wind, but it also needs someone who does not give up.”
>
> We tried again. This time, the kite rose into the sky. It danced above the hill, and I could feel the string pull my hand. For a few minutes, I forgot everything. Then the string broke, and the kite flew away. I watched it until it disappeared.
>
> I was sad, but my father smiled. “We can build another one,” he said. “The important thing is that you **had learned** how to fly it.”
>
> That day, I learned that success is not about keeping the kite. It is about learning how to fly it.
