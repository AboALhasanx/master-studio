## 6. الكولوكيشن

1. consistent **with / to**
2. equipped **with / by**
3. rely **on / in**
4. capture **the mood / the photo**
5. profound **significance / importance**
6. extracurricular **activities / works**
7. innate **talent / skill**
8. select **a course / a choice**

**إجابات:** 1 with · 2 with · 3 on · 4 the mood · 5 significance · 6 activities · 7 talent · 8 a course

## 7. أخطاء شائعة

| الخطأ | التصحيح | ليش |
|:--|:--|:--|
| ❌ consistent **to** his values | ✅ consistent **with** | حرف الجر |
| ❌ equipped **by** technology | ✅ equipped **with** | حرف الجر |
| ❌ rely **in** someone | ✅ rely **on** someone | حرف الجر |
| ❌ She had **went** to school. | ✅ had **gone** | past participle |
| ❌ He is **an** university student. | ✅ **a** university student | صوت /j/ |
| ❌ I **suspect** that... (بمعنى أشك سلباً) | ✅ **suspect** = أعتقد/أشك؛ **doubt** = أشك بعدم اليقين | فرق دلالي |

## ✎ أسئلة — استرجاع

1. اكتب 5 جمل: assumption · competence · nurture · capture · rely on.
2. شنو الفرق بين **suspect** و **doubt**؟
3. أعطِ كولوكيشنين لكلمة **significance**.
4. اكتب جملة فيها **consistent with**.
5. اكتب فقرة قصيرة (4 جمل) عن درس تعلمته في طفولتك.

**إجابات نموذجية:**

2. **suspect** = أعتقد أن شيئاً صحيح (غالباً سلبي)؛ **doubt** = أشك في صحة شيء.
3. the significance of · profound significance.
4. Her actions are **consistent with** her words.
5. نموذج: When I was a child, my father taught me to be patient. He **nurtured** my curiosity and always encouraged me to ask questions. That **period** of my life was important because it gave me **competence** and confidence. I **rely on** those lessons even today.
