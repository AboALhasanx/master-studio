# الكتابة — Narrative Essay

> **النوع:** مقال سردي (narrative essay) عن ذكرى من الطفولة.
> **الطول:** 4–5 فقرات، 250–350 كلمة.
> **الهدف:** تحكي قصة، وتوضح شنو تعلمت.

---

## 1. الهيكل

| الفقرة | الوظيفة |
|:--|:--|
| **Introduction** | who/where/when + ليش الذكرى مهمة |
| **Body 1** | الحدث الأول + تفاصيل |
| **Body 2** | نقطة التحول + تفاصيل |
| **Body 3** | النتيجة + تفاصيل |
| **Conclusion** | شنو تعلمت + ليش مهم اليوم |

**Time words:** when · while · after · before · by the time · as · until · later · the next day.

---

## 2. موديل — مقال سردي

> **The Day I Learned to Ride a Bike**
>
> When I was seven years old, my father decided to teach me how to ride a bicycle. I still remember the day clearly: it was a warm Friday morning, and the street was quiet. I was excited, but I was also afraid. I **had tried** to ride my cousin's bike before, and I **had fallen** twice.
>
> My father did not laugh at my fear. He held the back of the seat and said, “I will not let go until you are ready.” I started to pedal. The bike moved slowly, and I could hear my father's footsteps behind me. After a few minutes, I began to feel more confident. I **had never felt** so free.
>
> Then, without warning, my father let go. I did not know it at first. I kept pedaling, and the wind touched my face. When I turned my head, I saw that he was standing far behind me. I was riding alone! A moment later, I lost my balance and fell. My knee hurt, and I wanted to cry. My father ran to me, helped me up, and said, “You **had already ridden** before you fell. That is what matters.”
>
> I got back on the bike and tried again. By the time the sun went down, I **had fallen** three more times, but I could ride without help. I felt proud, not because I was perfect, but because I had not stopped.
>
> That day taught me a lesson that I still use today. Failure is not the opposite of success; it is part of it. My father did not give me a bicycle; he gave me the **resolve** to get up again. When I face a difficult problem now, I remember that warm Friday morning and tell myself, “You **had already ridden** before you fell.”

**الطول:** ~330 كلمة.

## 8. تدريب مؤقت — 40 دقيقة

### Prompt 1

> Write a narrative essay about a memory of a person who influenced you when you were younger. Explain what you learned.

**نموذج مصغر (فقرة):**

> I still remember my grandfather's garden. When I was eight, he **had already planted** more than fifty trees. Every Friday, he woke me up early and said, “Come, we have work to do.” I did not understand why he loved the garden so much. **By the time** I was twelve, I **had learned** how to plant, water, and wait. He taught me patience without saying a word.

### Prompt 2

> Write a narrative essay about a time you failed at something. Explain what happened and what you learned.

**نموذج مصغر:**

> When I was in the fifth grade, I failed my first math exam. I **had studied** for two days, but I **had not understood** the fractions. I felt **resentful** and wanted to give up. My teacher asked me to stay after class. She explained the lesson again, slowly. **After** she **had shown** me three examples, I understood. The next exam was much better.

## 9. إنشاء وزاري — 15 درجة

> **الموضوع:** Write an essay (120–180 words) about **a childhood memory that taught you a lesson**.
> **التعليمات:** اذكر المكان والزمان، الأحداث بالترتيب، والدرس الذي تعلمته.

**نموذج:**

> One of the most important memories of my childhood is the day I broke my mother's favorite vase. I was eight years old, and I was playing football in the living room. My mother **had told** me many times not to play inside, but I did not listen.
>
> **When** the ball hit the vase, it fell and broke into pieces. I was terrified. I **had never seen** my mother so angry. I tried to hide the pieces, but she found them. She did not shout. She sat down and said, “Things can be replaced. Trust cannot.”
>
> I felt **resentful** at first. I thought she was being too serious about a vase. But later, I understood. She was not angry about the vase; she was disappointed because I had not listened. **After** I **had apologized**, we cleaned the pieces together.
>
> That day taught me a lesson that I have never forgotten: honesty and responsibility are more important than any object. I still remember my mother's words, and I try to live by them.

**الطول:** ~170 كلمة.

---

## ✎ أسئلة — استرجاع

1. شنو وظيفة introduction في القصة؟
2. اذكر 3 time words.
3. صحّح: *I had visited her last week.*
4. صحّح: *She had went home.*
5. اكتب جملة فيها past perfect عن طفولتك.

**إجابات:**

1. تحدد who/where/when + ليش الذكرى مهمة.
2. when · after · by the time.
3. I **visited** her last week.
4. She had **gone** home.
5. نموذج: I **had learned** to swim before I started school.
