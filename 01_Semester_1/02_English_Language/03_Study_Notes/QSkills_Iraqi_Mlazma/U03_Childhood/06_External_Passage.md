# قطعة خارجية — What Failure Teaches Children

> **قطعة أصلية** بأسئلة بصيغة الامتحان. الوقت المقترح: 15 دقيقة.

---

## 1. اقرأ القطعة

Many parents want to protect their children from failure. They help with homework, call the teacher when a grade is low, and try to solve every problem. But some psychologists say that this **protective** style can be harmful. Children who never fail may never learn how to **confront** **adversity**.

A famous study by Carol Dweck, a psychologist at Stanford University, found that children who believe that **competence** is **innate** often give up when they face a difficult task. They think, “If I were smart, this would be easy.” Children who believe that ability can be **nurtured** through effort, on the other hand, keep trying. They see failure as **a** **period** of learning, not as **a** final judgment.

Dweck calls this difference a **growth mindset** versus **a fixed mindset**. In **a** growth mindset, **the** goal is not to look smart; **the** goal is to improve. **A** child with **a** growth mindset is more likely to **select** challenging tasks, **rely on** **a** strategy, and ask for help when needed.

This does not mean that parents should let children fail alone. **The** best support is **a** balance: **a** parent who is **consistent with** **the** message that effort matters, who provides **a** safe **refuge** after **a** failure, and who helps **the** child understand **the** **significance** of **the** experience.

**Theoretically**, every failure is **a** lesson. In practice, **a** child needs **an** adult who can turn **the** lesson into **a** story of growth. That adult does not need to be perfect. They only need to be present.

**H) نموذج:**

> When I failed my first driving test, I felt that I was not good at driving. My father did not criticize me. He **nurtured** my confidence and helped me practice every day. By the time I took the test again, I **had practiced** for three weeks. I passed. I learned that **competence** is built, not given.

## ✎ أسئلة — استرجاع

1. شنو growth mindset؟
2. شنو الفرق بين growth وfixed mindset؟
3. ليش الطفل يحتاج adult بعد الفشل؟
4. اكتب جملة فيها past perfect.
5. شنو معنى "only needs to be present"؟

**إجابات:**

1. الإيمان إن القدرة تتطور بالجهد.
2. growth: يحاول ويتعلم؛ fixed: يستسلم لأن الفشل يعني انه مو ذكي.
3. لأنه يحتاج حد يحول الفشل إلى درس.
4. نموذج: I **had finished** the book before the class started.
5. يحتاج شخصاً موجوداً/داعماً، مو شخصاً كاملاً.
