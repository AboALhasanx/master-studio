# الكتابة — Descriptive Essay

> **النوع:** مقال وصفي (descriptive essay) يراجع منتجاً أو خدمة.
> **الطول:** 4 فقرات، 250–350 كلمة.
> **الهدف:** تعطي القارئ صورة حسية واضحة + رأيك النهائي.

---

## 1. الهيكل

| الفقرة | الوظيفة |
|:--|:--|
| **Introduction** | جذب الانتباه + thesis statement (رأيك العام) |
| **Body 1** | الميزة الأولى + تفاصيل حسية |
| **Body 2** | الميزة الثانية + تفاصيل حسية |
| **Conclusion** | الرأي النهائي + توصية |

**الحواس الخمس:** sight · sound · smell · taste · touch.

---

## 2. موديل — مقال وصفي

> **The Small Bakery on Our Street**
>
> The small bakery on the corner of our street is my favourite place to buy breakfast because of its freshness, its prices, and its atmosphere. It is not a famous shop, and it does not have a big sign, but everyone in the neighbourhood knows it.
>
> As soon as you open the door, **the** rich smell of bread fills the air. **The** shop is small and warm, with **a** wooden counter and **a** few old shelves. **The** baker works behind **the** counter, and you can hear **the** soft sound of **the** oven and **the** quiet hum of **an** old radio. **The** bread is baked three times a day, so **the** loaves are always warm. When you touch them, **the** crust is crisp, and **the** inside is soft and light.
>
> **The** prices are another reason I keep coming back. **A** full breakfast — bread, cheese, and tea — costs less than **a** cup of coffee in **the** city centre. **The** owner knows his customers by name, and he always asks about **the** family. This creates **a** friendly mood that you cannot find in **a** large supermarket. **The** only negative is that **the** shop closes at two o'clock, so you cannot buy bread in the evening.
>
> In short, **the** small bakery is not just **a** place to buy food. It is **a** place where **the** community meets. Anyone who wants fresh bread and **a** warm welcome should stop, take **a** look, and go in. **The** smell alone is worth **the** trip.

**الطول:** ~290 كلمة.

---

## 3. تحليل الموديل

| العنصر | المثال | ملاحظة |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Thesis | ...because of its freshness, its prices, and its atmosphere. | ثلاث ميزات |
| Sensory — smell | the rich smell of bread | حاسة الشم |
| Sensory — sound | the soft sound of the oven / the quiet hum of an old radio | حاسة السمع |
| Sensory — touch | the crust is crisp, the inside is soft | حاسة اللمس |
| Sensory — sight | a wooden counter, old shelves, warm light | حاسة البصر |
| Contrast | costs less than a cup of coffee in the city centre | مقارنة |
| Negative | the only negative is that the shop closes at two | توازن |
| Conclusion | Anyone who wants... should stop, take a look, and go in. | توصية |

## 5. عبارات مفيدة

### الوصف الحسي

- The smell of... fills the air.
- You can hear the sound of...
- The... feels soft / crisp / smooth.
- The light is warm / bright / soft.
- It tastes fresh / rich / sweet.

### العرض

- First, ... · Second, ... · Another reason is...
- For example, ... · For instance, ...
- What I like most is...
- The only negative is...

### الخاتمة

- In short, ...
- For these reasons, ...
- Anyone who... should...

## 6. أخطاء شائعة

| الخطأ | التصحيح | ليش |
|:--|:--|:--|
| ❌ The shop is **a** small shop. | ✅ The shop is **a** small place. | تكرار shop |
| ❌ I bought **a** bread. | ✅ I bought **Ø** bread. | غير معدود |
| ❌ **The** life is beautiful. | ✅ **Ø** Life is beautiful. | عام |
| ❌ She is **an** university student. | ✅ **a** university student. | صوت /j/ |
| ❌ The prices are **cheaper** than... | ✅ The prices are **lower** than... | price → low/high |
| ❌ It **gives** a good impression. | ✅ It **makes** a good impression. | collocation |

## 7. Rubric — 20 درجة

| المعيار | الوزن | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|:--|:--:|:--|:--|:--|
| Thesis + introduction | 20% | واضح + جاذب | واضح | غائب |
| Sensory details | 30% | 5 حواس | 3 حواس | 1 حاسة |
| Organization | 20% | 4 فقرات مترابطة | 3 فقرات | فقرة واحدة |
| Grammar/articles | 15% | صفر أخطاء | 2–3 | 4+ |
| Vocabulary | 10% | 5+ كلمات | 3 | 1 |
| Mechanics | 5% | صفر أخطاء | 2–3 | 4+ |

**الهدف:** 16/20 أو أكثر.

---

## 8. تدريب مؤقت — 40 دقيقة

### Prompt 1

> Write a descriptive essay (4 paragraphs) reviewing a restaurant or café you know well. Describe the food, the atmosphere, and the service, and give your final opinion.

**نموذج مصغر (فقرة واحدة):**

> The café on the main street is a good place to study because it is quiet, comfortable, and cheap. **The** lights are soft, **the** chairs are comfortable, and **the** music is low. **The** smell of coffee fills **the** room, and **the** staff never ask you to leave. **A** cup of tea costs very little, and **the** owner always smiles. **The** only negative is that it closes early on Fridays.

### Prompt 2

> Write a descriptive essay reviewing a product you bought recently (a phone, a laptop, a bag, etc.). Describe its appearance, its features, and your experience with it.

**نموذج مصغر:**

> I bought **a** new backpack last month, and it is one of the best purchases I have made. **The** bag is dark blue, with **a** strong zipper and **a** soft lining. **The** straps are wide, so **the** weight feels light on my shoulders. **The** only negative is that **the** front pocket is too small for **a** water bottle. In short, it is **a** practical bag for **a** student who carries books every day.

---

## 9. إنشاء وزاري — 15 درجة

> **الموضوع:** Write an essay (120–180 words) reviewing **a shop or restaurant you like**.
> **التعليمات:** اذكر المكان، وصفه بحواسك، اذكر ميزتين، وقل رأيك النهائي.

**نموذج:**

> The small restaurant at the end of our street is my favourite place to eat. It is not a famous restaurant, but it is always full of people who live in the neighbourhood.
>
> The first thing you notice is **the** smell. **The** kitchen is open, and **the** smell of grilled meat and fresh bread fills **the** air. **The** tables are simple, but **the** chairs are comfortable, and **the** light is warm. You can hear **the** sound of conversation and **the** quiet music in **the** background.
>
> **The** food is another reason to come. **The** grilled chicken is juicy, **the** rice is soft, and **the** salad is fresh. **A** full meal costs less than **a** meal in **a** big restaurant, and **the** owner always gives **a** free cup of tea.
>
> In short, this restaurant is not just **a** place to eat. It is **a** place where **the** community meets. Anyone who wants good food and **a** warm welcome should visit it.

**الطول:** ~160 كلمة.

---

## ✎ أسئلة — استرجاع

1. شنو thesis statement؟
2. اذكر 3 عبارات حسية.
3. صحّح: *I bought a bread.*
4. صحّح: *She is an university student.*
5. اكتب thesis statement عن مطعم تحبه.

**إجابات:**

1. جملة تحدد رأيك العام + سبب اختيارك.
2. the smell of... · the sound of... · the... feels soft/crisp.
3. I bought **Ø** bread.
4. She is **a** university student.
5. نموذج: The restaurant on our street is the best place for lunch because of its fresh food, low prices, and friendly staff.
