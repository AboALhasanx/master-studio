## Section 3 — Reading (10 × 1)

### اقرأ القطعة ثم أجب

> **The Shop With No Prices**
>
> In a small street in Baghdad, there is **a** bakery with no prices on the wall. **The** owner, Mr. Sami, says, “People pay what they can.” **The** idea is simple: **a** customer takes **a** loaf of bread and leaves **some** money in **a** box. No one checks. No one asks.
>
> At first, people thought **the** idea would fail. “Everyone will take **the** bread and pay nothing,” they said. But **the** opposite happened. Most customers paid **the** normal price, and some paid more. **A** few paid less, but **the** bakery still made enough money to stay open.
>
> Mr. Sami believes that **the** system works because it treats people with respect. “When you trust people, they usually deserve it,” he says. **The** shop has become popular, and **the** queue is often long. **The** customers say that **the** bread tastes better when you know that **the** money goes to **a** good place.
>
> **The** experiment is not perfect. **A** few people take advantage of **the** system. But Mr. Sami says that **the** good people are more than **the** bad ones. “**A** shop is not only about money,” he says. “It is about **the** community.”
