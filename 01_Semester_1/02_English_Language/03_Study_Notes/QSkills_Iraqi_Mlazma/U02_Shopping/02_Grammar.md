# القواعد — Articles: a / an / the / Ø

> **القاعدة في سطر:** a/an لأول ذكر غير محدد · the للمعروف أو الوحيد · Ø للجمع العام وغير المعدود العام.

---

## 1. الخريطة

| النوع | مفرد معدود | جمع معدود | غير معدود |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **غير محدد** | a / an | Ø | Ø |
| **محدد** | the | the | the |

**أمثلة:**

- I bought **a** table. *(أول ذكر)*
- **The** table is in the kitchen. *(معروف)*
- **Ø** Tables are expensive. *(جمع عام)*
- **Ø** Information is important. *(غير معدود عام)*

---

## 2. a / an — حسب الصوت، مو الحرف

| الصوت | الأداة | أمثلة |
|:--|:--|:--|
| صوت ساكن | **a** | a book · a university (/juː/) · a European · a one-way street (/w/) |
| صوت علة | **an** | an apple · an hour (h صامتة) · an MBA (/em/) · an honest man |

| ❌ خطأ | ✅ صحيح | السبب |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **an** university | **a** university | صوت /j/ |
| **a** hour | **an** hour | h صامتة |
| **an** European | **a** European | صوت /j/ |

---

## 3. the — متى؟

1. **ذُكر سابقاً:** I bought a table. **The** table is nice.
2. **معروف من السياق:** **The** kitchen is small. *(مطبخ البيت)*
3. **وحيد:** **the** Internet · **the** weather · **the** government · **the** sun.
4. **صفة عليا:** **the** best · **the** most important.
5. **أسماء جغرافية:** **the** Tigris · **the** Gulf · **the** United States. *(لكن: Iraq, Baghdad, Mount Everest بلا the)*
6. **جملة وصل تحدد الاسم:** **the** job **that I told you about**.

---

## 4. Ø — متى؟

1. **جمع عام:** **Ø** Buyers want good prices.
2. **غير معدود عام:** **Ø** Information is useful. · **Ø** Life is difficult.
3. **أسماء العلم:** **Ø** Iraq · **Ø** Baghdad · **Ø** English.
4. **الوجبات/الرياضة/المواد:** **Ø** breakfast · **Ø** football · **Ø** mathematics.
5. **تعبيرات ثابتة:** go to **Ø** school (للدراسة) · go to **Ø** bed · at **Ø** home · at **Ø** work · by **Ø** car.

> **ملاحظة:** go to **the** school = تروح للمبنى، مو للدراسة.

---

## 5. أخطاء شائعة

| الخطأ | التصحيح | ليش |
|:--|:--|:--|
| ❌ **a** information | ✅ **Ø** information | غير معدود |
| ❌ **an** university | ✅ **a** university | صوت /j/ |
| ❌ **the** life is hard | ✅ **Ø** Life is hard | عام |
| ❌ I go to **the** school every day. | ✅ I go to **Ø** school every day. | للدراسة |
| ❌ **The** Iraq is my country. | ✅ **Ø** Iraq is my country. | اسم علم |
| ❌ She is **the** teacher. *(أول ذكر)* | ✅ She is **a** teacher. | غير محدد |

## 6. تمارين

### A) أكمل الإيميل — 19 فراغاً

> Hi Felix,
>
> Sorry I haven't been in touch for a while, but I have (1) ___ big news. I got (2) ___ job that I told you about, so I'm selling my house! You know that (3) ___ housing market is very tough right now, but I'm happy because there is already (4) ___ couple who are interested in my place. (5) ___ potential buyers are coming over in two weeks. I want to stage (6) ___ house so that everything looks perfect. This weekend, I'm going to clean (7) ___ house all over and repaint all (8) ___ doors. I also bought (9) ___ awesome dining table to put in (10) ___ kitchen. Then I want to paint (11) ___ main bedroom — probably in (12) ___ soft blue color. And when (13) ___ weather is nice, I want to clean up (14) ___ yard and plant (15) ___ flowers. Real estate agents always say that when (16) ___ buyers are looking at (17) ___ houses, they want to feel like they already live there, so I know this will be worth all (18) ___ effort. Do you know where I can find (19) ___ good hardware store?
>
> Louisa

**إجابات A:** 1 Ø · 2 the · 3 the · 4 a · 5 The · 6 the · 7 the · 8 the · 9 an · 10 the · 11 the · 12 a · 13 the · 14 the · 15 Ø · 16 Ø · 17 Ø · 18 the · 19 a

> **ليش فراغ 2 = the؟** لأن *that I told you about* تحدد الوظيفة ← صارت معروفة. مو *a*.

## ✎ أسئلة — استرجاع

1. اكتب 3 أسماء غير معدودة.
2. اكتب 3 أسماء جمع عامة.
3. صحّح: *She is an university student.*
4. صحّح: *The life is hard.*
5. اكتب جملتين: واحدة فيها a، وواحدة فيها the.

**إجابات:**

1. information · water · advice.
2. books · buyers · houses.
3. She is **a** university student.
4. **Ø** Life is hard.
5. نموذج: I bought **a** book. **The** book is interesting.
