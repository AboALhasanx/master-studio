## 7. أخطاء شائعة

| الخطأ | التصحيح | ليش |
|:--|:--|:--|
| ❌ He promoted **to** the product. | ✅ He **promoted** the product. | promote + object |
| ❌ She alluded **the** problem. | ✅ She alluded **to** the problem. | allude to |
| ❌ I focus **in** my study. | ✅ I focus **on** my study. | focus on |
| ❌ The store distinguishes **from** others. | ✅ The store distinguishes **itself from** others. | reflexive |
| ❌ **a** information | ✅ **Ø** information | غير معدود |
| ❌ **an** university | ✅ **a** university | صوت /j/ |

## ✎ أسئلة — استرجاع

1. اكتب 5 جمل: pedestrians · concept · focus · priorities · liberate.
2. شنو الفرق بين **feature** (فعل) و **feature** (اسم)؟
3. أعطِ كولوكيشنين لكلمة **attention**.
4. اكتب جملة فيها **in theory**.
5. اكتب فقرة قصيرة (4 جمل) عن محل تحبه، واستخدم 4 كلمات من الوحدة.

**إجابات نموذجية:**

2. **feature** (v.) = يبرز/يعرض؛ **feature** (n.) = صفة/خاصية.
3. grab attention · catch attention.
4. **In theory**, the bus takes 20 minutes, but it usually takes 40.
5. نموذج: The small shop near my house is my favourite place. It **distinguishes** itself by its low prices and friendly staff. I **focus** on the quality of the products, not the advertising. My **priorities** are quality and price, and this shop gives me both.
