# الكتابة — Analysis Paragraph

> **النوع:** فقرة تحليلية (analysis paragraph).
> **السؤال:** What makes someone a hero?
> **الطول:** 120–180 كلمة.
> **الهدف:** تحلل صفات البطل، مو تسرد قصة.

---

## 1. الهيكل

| الجزء | الوظيفة | مثال |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Topic sentence** | فكرة رئيسية + controlling idea | A hero is not defined by superpowers, but by the willingness to act for others. |
| **Supporting sentence 1** | سبب + مثال | First, heroes confront adversity... |
| **Supporting sentence 2** | سبب + مثال | Second, they embody sacrifice... |
| **Supporting sentence 3** | سبب + مثال | Finally, they inspire others... |
| **Concluding sentence** | إعادة صياغة + خلاصة | In short, heroism is a decision, not a power. |

---

## 2. موديل — فقرة تحليلية

> **A Hero Is a Decision**
>
> A hero is not defined by superpowers, but by the willingness to act for others. First, heroes **confront adversity** even when they are afraid. For example, a firefighter **who** enters a burning building does not have superpowers; he has courage and a sense of duty. Second, heroes **embody** sacrifice. They put the needs of others before their own comfort, as parents do when they work long hours so that their children can study. Finally, heroes inspire the people **who** see them. A teacher **who** helps a poor student does more than teach a lesson; she shows the whole class that kindness is possible. In short, heroism is not a power that falls from the sky. It is a decision that ordinary people make every day.

**الطول:** ~135 كلمة.

## 8. تدريب مؤقت — 25 دقيقة

### Prompt 1

> Write a paragraph about a person **who** you consider a hero. Explain **why** this person is a hero, and give **examples** of what he or she does.

**نموذج:**

> My grandfather is a hero to me because he has devoted his life to helping others. First, he **confronts** **adversity** without complaining. When he lost his job, he did not give up; he opened a small shop and worked every day to support his family. Second, he **embodies** generosity. He gives food to poor neighbors and never asks for anything back. Finally, he inspires me to **pursue** my goals. He always says, “A person **who** helps others is never poor.” For these reasons, my grandfather is the hero **who** shaped my life.

### Prompt 2

> Write a paragraph about a hero **who** is not famous. Explain what makes this person heroic.

**نموذج:**

> Not all heroes appear on television. My neighbor, Mrs. Layla, is a hero **who** works quietly. First, she **is acknowledged for** her patience. She teaches children **who** cannot afford private lessons, and she never charges them. Second, she **confronts** problems that other people ignore. When the street was dirty, she organized a cleanup group and **convened** the neighbors every Saturday. Finally, she **nurtures** confidence in young people. She tells them that their **achievements** matter, even if no one sees them. In short, Mrs. Layla proves that heroism is a daily choice, not a famous act.

## 9. إنشاء وزاري — 15 درجة

> **الموضوع:** Write an essay (120–180 words) about **a person you admire**.
> **التعليمات:** اذكر من هو، لماذا admireه، وأعطِ مثالين.

**نموذج:**

> The person I admire most is my mother. She is not famous, and she has never had an easy life, but she is the strongest person I know.
>
> First, my mother **confronts** **adversity** with patience. When my father was ill, she worked long hours and took care of the family at the same time. She never complained, and she never let us feel afraid. Second, she **embodies** sacrifice. She always puts our needs before her own. For example, she gave up her own studies so that my brother and I could go to university. Finally, she **nurtures** our dreams. She listens to us, encourages us, and reminds us that **achievement** comes from hard work.
>
> My mother is a hero **who** does not wear a cape. She is a hero **who** wakes up early, works hard, and loves her family without limits. I **aspire to** be like her, and I hope that one day I can give her the life **that** she deserves.

**الطول:** ~170 كلمة.

---

## ✎ أسئلة — استرجاع

1. شنو وظيفة topic sentence؟
2. اكتب topic sentence عن بطل تحبه.
3. اذكر 3 روابط تستخدمها في الفقرة.
4. صحّح: *A hero is someone which helps people.*
5. اكتب concluding sentence لفقرة عن أبيك.

**إجابات:**

1. تحدد الفكرة الرئيسية + controlling idea.
2. نموذج: A hero is a person who puts others before himself.
3. First · Second · Finally (أو For example · In addition · In short).
4. A hero is someone **who/that** helps people.
5. نموذج: For these reasons, my father is the hero who taught me the value of hard work.
