# القواعد — Restrictive Relative Clauses

> **القاعدة في سطر:** who/that للأشخاص، which/that للأشياء. الضمير يُحذف فقط إذا كان **مفعولاً** داخل الجملة النسبية.

---

## 1. شنو الجملة النسبية؟

الجملة النسبية (relative clause) جملة صغيرة **توصف اسم** قبلها. تجي بعد الاسم مباشرة.

**مثال:**
> A hero is someone **who embodies the best human qualities**.
> (A hero is someone. + He embodies the best human qualities.)

- الاسم: **someone**
- الجملة النسبية: **who embodies the best human qualities**
- الضمير: **who** يعود على someone.

---

## 2. أدوات الربط

| الاسم | الأداة | مثال |
|:--|:--|:--|
| شخص | **who / that** | A hero is a person **who** performs great acts. |
| شيء | **which / that** | Comic books **that** tell stories about superheroes are popular. |
| شخص (مفعول) | **who / that / Ø** | The man **(who/that/Ø)** I met was kind. |
| شيء (مفعول) | **which / that / Ø** | The book **(which/that/Ø)** I bought was expensive. |

> **ملاحظة:** **which** أكثر رسمية من **that** في الكتابة الأكاديمية.

---

## 3. Subject vs Object

### (أ) Subject relative clause

الضمير هو **فاعل** الجملة النسبية ← بعده **فعل** مباشرة. **لا يُحذف**.

> A hero is someone **who embodies** good qualities.
> (who = subject; embodies = verb)

### (ب) Object relative clause

الضمير هو **مفعول** الجملة النسبية ← بعده **فاعل + فعل**. **يُحذف**.

> Superheroes face questions **that we will face** someday.
> (that = object; we = subject; will face = verb)

**بعد الحذف:**
> Superheroes face questions **we will face** someday.

---

## 4. متى نحذف الضمير؟

| الحالة | مثال | حذف؟ |
|:--|:--|:--:|
| الضمير فاعل | The man **who lives** next door is a doctor. | ❌ لا |
| الضمير مفعول | The book **that I bought** is expensive. | ✅ نعم |
| الضمير مفعول + حرف جر | The house **that we live in** is old. | ✅ نعم (لكن لا نحذف الحرف) |

**اختبار سريع:** إذا بعد الضمير **فعل** ← فاعل ← لا تحذف. إذا بعد الضمير **اسم/ضمير فاعل** ← مفعول ← يمكن الحذف.

---

## 5. أخطاء شائعة

| الخطأ | التصحيح | ليش |
|:--|:--|:--|
| ❌ The man **which** helped me. | ✅ **who/that** | شخص |
| ❌ The book **who** I read. | ✅ **which/that** | شيء |
| ❌ The man **who he** helped me. | ✅ **who helped me** | لا نكرر الفاعل |
| ❌ The boy **lives** next door is my friend. | ✅ **who lives** | الضمير فاعل، لا يُحذف |
| ❌ This is the house **which I bought it**. | ✅ **which I bought** | لا نكرر المفعول |
| ❌ The people **which** came late. | ✅ **who/that** | أشخاص |
| ❌ The film **who** we watched. | ✅ **which/that** | شيء |
| ❌ I met a girl **who father** is a doctor. | ✅ **whose father** | الملكية ← whose |

## 6. تمارين

### A) حدّد الجملة النسبية والاسم الذي تصفه

1. Not every character that has superpowers is necessarily a superhero.
2. Some superheroes possess abilities which they have developed to a superhuman level.
3. At 19, Yohannes borrowed a novel that changed his life forever.
4. His father was an illiterate cattle merchant who insisted that his son have an education.
5. He reads storybooks to children who have no access to television.
6. Salva started a youth environmental group which is trying to clean up the city.
7. The trash Salva's group collects is carried away by llamas.

**إجابات A:**

1. clause: **that has superpowers** — noun: **character**
2. clause: **which they have developed to a superhuman level** — noun: **abilities**
3. clause: **that changed his life forever** — noun: **novel**
4. clause: **who insisted that his son have an education** — noun: **merchant**
5. clause: **who have no access to television** — noun: **children**
6. clause: **which is trying to clean up the city** — noun: **group**
7. clause: **(that) Salva's group collects** — noun: **trash** (الضمير محذوف لأنه مفعول)

### B) ادمج الجملتين باستخدام who / that / which

1. We all aspire to do something. Other people will respect it.
2. A superhero helps people. They cannot help themselves.
3. Superheroes engage in activities. We would like to experience them.
4. To me, a person is a hero. He inspires others to do good deeds.
5. Reading books for pleasure gives students something. They cannot get it in regular textbooks.
6. Caring for the environment is something. We can all do it.
7. Someone is a generous person. He or she donates money to charity.

**إجابات B:**

1. We all aspire to do something **that other people will respect**.
2. A superhero helps people **who/that cannot help themselves**.
3. Superheroes engage in activities **that/which we would like to experience**.
4. To me, a person **who/that inspires others to do good deeds** is a hero.
5. Reading books for pleasure gives students something **that/which they cannot get in regular textbooks**.
6. Caring for the environment is something **that/which we can all do**.
7. Someone **who/that donates money to charity** is a generous person.

### C) أي الجمل يمكن حذف الضمير منها؟

1. The book that I bought was expensive.
2. The man who called you is my uncle.
3. The film which we watched was boring.
4. The teacher who teaches us English is kind.
5. The house that we live in is old.
6. The girl who won the prize is my sister.
7. The ideas that they presented were interesting.

**إجابات C:** يمكن الحذف في **1، 3، 5، 7** (الضمير مفعول). لا يمكن في **2، 4، 6** (الضمير فاعل).

### D) صحّح الخطأ

1. The man which helped me was kind.
2. The book who I read was interesting.
3. The woman who she called me is my aunt.
4. The boy lives next door is my friend.
5. This is the house which I bought it last year.
6. The people which came late missed the bus.
7. The film who we watched was boring.
8. The teacher which teaches us English is from Iraq.

**إجابات D:**

1. The man **who/that** helped me was kind.
2. The book **which/that** I read was interesting.
3. The woman **who called** me is my aunt.
4. The boy **who lives** next door is my friend.
5. This is the house **which I bought** last year.
6. The people **who/that** came late missed the bus.
7. The film **which/that** we watched was boring.
8. The teacher **who/that** teaches us English is from Iraq.

### F) أكمل الجمل بأفكارك

1. A hero is someone who ________.
2. I want to read a book that ________.
3. The person who ________ is my friend.
4. This is the place which ________.
5. People who ________ should be respected.
6. She bought a dress that ________.
7. The students who ________ passed the exam.
8. I know a man whose ________.

**إجابات F (نماذج):**

1. A hero is someone who **helps others without expecting anything back**.
2. I want to read a book that **explains how the brain works**.
3. The person who **lives next door** is my friend.
4. This is the place which **we visited last summer**.
5. People who **volunteer in their community** should be respected.
6. She bought a dress that **was made in Turkey**.
7. The students who **studied every day** passed the exam.
8. I know a man whose **son is a doctor**.

### G) تطبيق كتابي — ادمج عن البطل

1. A hero is a person. He helps others.
2. I admire a teacher. She teaches poor children.
3. She started a project. It changed the village.
4. The project gives children books. They cannot buy them.
5. The children love the teacher. She gives them hope.

**إجابات G:**

1. A hero is a person **who helps others**.
2. I admire a teacher **who teaches poor children**.
3. She started a project **that/which changed the village**.
4. The project gives children books **that/which they cannot buy**.
5. The children love the teacher **who gives them hope**.

## 7. خلاصة قبل الامتحان

| السؤال | الجواب |
|:--|:--|
| شخص ← | who / that |
| شيء ← | which / that |
| مفعول ← | يمكن حذف الضمير |
| فاعل ← | لا يُحذف |
| بعد الضمير فعل ← | فاعل |
| بعد الضمير اسم/ضمير ← | مفعول |

---

## ✎ أسئلة — استرجاع

1. اكتب جملة فيها subject relative clause.
2. اكتب جملة فيها object relative clause، واحذف الضمير.
3. صحّح: *The book who I read was good.*
4. صحّح: *The man who he helped me was kind.*
5. ليش ما نحذف الضمير في *The teacher who teaches us*؟

**إجابات:**

1. The teacher **who teaches us** is kind.
2. The book **I bought** was expensive.
3. The book **which/that** I read was good.
4. The man **who helped** me was kind.
5. لأن الضمير **فاعل** (بعده فعل teaches)، والفاعل لا يُحذف.
