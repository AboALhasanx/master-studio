## Section 3 — Reading (10 × 1)

### اقرأ القطعة ثم أجب

> **The Woman Who Planted Trees**
>
> In a small village, the land was dry and the summer sun was very strong. The children had no place to play in the shade. An old woman named Mrs. Amina decided to change this. She started planting trees around the school.
>
> At first, people thought she was wasting water. “The trees will die,” they said. “There is not enough rain.” Mrs. Amina did not argue. She collected rainwater in large containers and used it carefully. Every morning, she carried water to the young trees.
>
> After three years, more than 200 trees were growing around the school. The schoolyard became green, and the children could play outside without suffering from the heat. The villagers began to help her, and the project spread to other streets.
>
> “A hero is someone who plants a tree even if he will not sit in its shade,” Mrs. Amina says. “I may not see all these trees grow, but my grandchildren will.”

26. What is the main idea of the passage?
    a) Trees need a lot of water.
    b) One woman's patience changed a village.
    c) Children should not play outside.
    d) The village had too much rain.

27. Where did Mrs. Amina plant the trees?
    a) around the school  b) near the river  c) in her house  d) on the mountain

28. Why did people doubt her at first?
    a) They did not like trees.
    b) They thought there was not enough water.
    c) They wanted to leave the village.
    d) They thought she was too old.

29. How did she water the trees?
    a) She used a river.  b) She collected rainwater.  c) She bought water.  d) She asked the government.

30. What happened after three years?
    a) The trees died.  b) More than 200 trees were growing.  c) The school closed.  d) The village moved.

31. What can you infer about Mrs. Amina?
    a) She wanted to be famous.  b) She cared about the future.  c) She was rich.  d) She disliked children.

32. What does “even if he will not sit in its shade” mean?
    a) A hero works only for himself.
    b) A hero helps future generations.
    c) A hero needs shade.
    d) A hero plants trees quickly.

33. The word **dry** is closest in meaning to:
    a) wet  b) without water  c) cold  d) green

34. The word **shade** means:
    a) a place protected from the sun  b) a type of tree  c) a strong wind  d) a kind of water

35. What is the writer's main purpose?
    a) To criticize the village.
    b) To show how one person can make a difference.
    c) To explain how to plant trees.
    d) To describe the weather.

## Section 5 — Writing (5 × 1)

### اختر موضوعاً واحداً واكتب 100–150 كلمة

**A)** Write a paragraph about a person **who** you consider a hero. Explain why, and give examples.

**B)** Write a paragraph about a hero **who** is not famous.

**C)** Write a paragraph about a time when you helped someone. What did you learn?

**D)** Write a paragraph about a person **who** influenced your life.
