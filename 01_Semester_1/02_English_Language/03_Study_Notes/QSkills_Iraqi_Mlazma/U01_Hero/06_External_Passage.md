# قطعة خارجية — The Book Box

> **قطعة أصلية** بأسئلة بصيغة الامتحان. ليست من الكتاب. الوقت المقترح: 15 دقيقة.

---

## 1. اقرأ القطعة

In a poor neighborhood of Baghdad, there is a small shop that does not sell anything. Its door is open every afternoon, and children walk in, take a book from the shelf, and sit on the floor to read. The shop is called **The Book Box**, and it is a library.

The project began three years ago. A group of university students noticed that the children in the area had no **access** to books. The nearest public library was far away, and most families could not afford to buy books. The students had a **vision**: a place where any child could read for free.

They found an old shop that nobody used, cleaned it, painted the walls, and asked people for books. Many families **allocated** a few books from their homes. Within two months, the shelves were full. The students **convened** every Friday to organize the books and plan activities.

At first, very few children came. Then a girl named Sara started visiting every day. She read stories to her younger brothers at home, and soon other children wanted to come too. **Ultimately**, more than 300 **participants** joined the project. University volunteers now help the children with their homework and teach them how to read difficult words.

The project is not expensive, but it needs **primary** things: books, lights, and people who care. The students do not receive money for their work. They say that the smile of a child who finishes a book is enough.

“A hero is not someone with a cape,” says Ali, one of the founders. “A hero is someone **who** opens a door for others. We just opened a door. The children did the rest.”

## 2. الأسئلة

### A) Main Idea

1. What is the main idea of the passage?
   a) Baghdad has many public libraries.
   b) A group of students created a free library for children.
   c) Books are expensive in Iraq.
   d) University students need money.

### B) Details

2. Where is The Book Box?
3. Why did the students start the project?
4. How did they get the books?
5. How often did the students convene?
6. How many children joined the project?

### C) Inference

7. Why did more children come after Sara?
8. Why do the students refuse to receive money?
9. What does Ali mean by “We just opened a door”?

### D) Vocabulary in Context

10. The word **access** is closest in meaning to:
    a) money  b) ability to reach  c) books  d) school

11. The word **convened** is closest in meaning to:
    a) separated  b) gathered  c) traveled  d) painted

12. The word **ultimately** is closest in meaning to:
    a) at first  b) in the end  c) never  d) suddenly

### E) Reference

13. The word **It** in “It is a library” refers to:
    a) the shop  b) the neighborhood  c) the door  d) the book

### F) Purpose

14. What is the writer's main purpose?
    a) To criticize the government.
    b) To show how ordinary people can create change.
    c) To sell books.
    d) To describe a university.

### G) Grammar

15. Underline the relative clause: “A hero is someone who opens a door for others.”
16. Combine: “The students started a project. It changed the neighborhood.”
17. Combine: “The children had no books. They came to the library.”

### H) Writing

18. Write a short paragraph (60–80 words) about a project that helps people in your community. Use **access**, **vision**, and **participants**.

**A)** 1-b

**D)** 10-b · 11-b · 12-b

**E)** 13-a

**F)** 14-b

**G)**

15. **who opens a door for others**
16. The students started a project **that/which changed the neighborhood**.
17. The children **who had no books** came to the library. *(أو: The children came to the library because they had no books — لكن المطلوب relative clause.)*

**H) نموذج:**

> In my neighborhood, a group of young people started a small **project** to teach children computer skills. Their **vision** was simple: every child should have **access** to a computer. At first, only five **participants** joined, but after a month, more than thirty children were coming every week. The project is free, and the volunteers say that helping a child learn is the best reward.
