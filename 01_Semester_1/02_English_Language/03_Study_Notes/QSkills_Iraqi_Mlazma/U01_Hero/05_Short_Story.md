# قصة قصيرة — The Man Who Fixed Bicycles

> **قصة أصلية** كُتبت للتدريب. تستخدم مفردات الوحدة وقاعدة relative clauses. ليست من الكتاب.

---

## 1. قبل القراءة

| الكلمة | عربي |
|:--|:--|
| **resolve** | عزم |
| **adversity** | محنة |
| **confront** | يواجه |

## 2. القصة

My town had many loud people, but Mr. Hameed was not one of them. He owned a small bicycle shop at the end of our street. He was a thin man with gray hair and hands that were always covered in oil. People did not **acknowledge** him for anything special. He was just the man who fixed bicycles.

But there was a **version** of Mr. Hameed that most people did not see. Every Friday, he opened his shop early and fixed the bicycles of children **who** could not pay. He never asked for money. He only asked one question: “Is this bike important to you?” If the child said yes, he smiled and began to work.

My friend Omar was one of those children. His father had lost his job, and the family was **constrained** by poverty. Omar’s bike was old and broken, but it was the only way he could get to school. Mr. Hameed fixed it three times that year. Each time, Omar tried to pay him with coins, and each time Mr. Hameed refused.

Then the flood came. It rained for three days, and the river at the edge of town broke its banks. Water filled the streets and entered the houses. People ran to the school on the hill, but the school was full. Mr. Hameed opened his shop and shouted, “Come in! There is space here!”

That night, forty people slept on the floor of the bicycle shop. Mr. Hameed gave them bread, blankets, and hot tea. When the water reached the old water pump behind the shop, he took his tools and repaired it in the rain. Without that pump, the families would have had no clean water.

In the morning, a journalist came to take photos. She asked Mr. Hameed, “Why did you do this? You are a hero.” He looked at her for a moment and said, “I am not a hero. I am a man **who** had a shop and a pump. A hero is someone **who** is there when it matters.”

The town did not forget him. After the flood, people **acknowledged** him for his help. They gave him a new sign for his shop. But Mr. Hameed did not change. He still opened early on Fridays, still fixed the bicycles of children **who** could not pay, and still asked the same question.

I learned something important from him. A hero does not need superpowers. A hero needs **resolve** when things are difficult, the courage to **confront** **adversity**, and the **inclination** to help others even when no one is watching.

## 3. أسئلة الفهم

### A) Main Idea

1. What is the main idea of the story?
   a) Bicycles are expensive.
   b) A quiet repairman becomes a hero through small acts of kindness.
   c) Floods are common in the town.
   d) Journalists are heroes.

### B) Details

2. What did Mr. Hameed do every Friday?
3. Why was Omar’s bike important to him?
4. What happened when the flood came?
5. What did Mr. Hameed repair in the rain?
6. What did the town give him after the flood?

### C) Inference

7. Why did Mr. Hameed refuse Omar’s coins?
8. What does Mr. Hameed mean by “A hero is someone who is there when it matters”?
9. Why did the journalist come?

### D) Vocabulary in Context

10. Find a word in the story that means “limited by something”.
11. Find a word that means “a difficult situation”.
12. Find a word that means “to face a problem”.

### E) Grammar — Relative Clauses

13. Underline the relative clause: “He fixed the bicycles of children who could not pay.”
14. Combine: “Mr. Hameed is a man. He had a shop and a pump.”
15. Combine: “The children could not pay. Mr. Hameed fixed their bicycles.”

### F) Writing

16. Write a paragraph (80–100 words) about a person in your life who is a hero to you. Use at least 4 words from the unit and one relative clause.

## 4. الإجابات

**A)** 1-b

**B)**

2. He opened his shop early and fixed the bicycles of children who could not pay.
3. It was the only way he could get to school.
4. The river broke its banks, water filled the streets and houses, and people ran to the school on the hill.
5. The old water pump behind the shop.
6. A new sign for his shop.

**C)**

7. Because he wanted to help, not to make money; he knew Omar’s family was poor.
8. A hero is not someone with special powers; a hero is someone who acts when help is needed.
9. To report on the flood and the people who helped.

**D)** 10. constrained · 11. adversity · 12. confront

**E)**

13. **who could not pay**
14. Mr. Hameed is a man **who had a shop and a pump**.
15. Mr. Hameed fixed the bicycles of children **who could not pay**. *(أو: The children whose bicycles Mr. Hameed fixed could not pay — لكن الأولى أبسط.)*

**F) نموذج:**

> My uncle is a hero to me. He is a teacher **who** works in a poor village. He is **constrained** by a small salary, but he never gives up. He **confronts** **adversity** every day and **pursues** his goal: to teach every child to read. He is **acknowledged for** his patience, and he **embodies** the idea that a hero is someone who helps others without expecting anything back.
