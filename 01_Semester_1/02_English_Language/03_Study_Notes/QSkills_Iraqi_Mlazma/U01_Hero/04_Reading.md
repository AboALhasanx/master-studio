## Reading 1 — We All Need a Hero

### 1. قبل القراءة — Preview

**العنوان:** We All Need a Hero
**المؤلفان:** Jeph Loeb & Tom Morris (مقتطف من *Superheroes and Philosophy*)

**توقّع:** النص راح يعرّف البطل الخارق، وليش نحتاجه، وشنو يعلّمنا.

### 2. ملخص عربي

الكتاب والفنانون اللي يخلقون الأبطال الخارقين يعتقدون إن هاي الشخصيات **تجسّد أعمق آمالنا ومخاوفنا**، وتمثّل أعلى طموحاتنا، وتساعدنا نواجه أسوأ كوابيسنا. البطل الخارق = قوى تتجاوز الإنسان العادي + **السعي إلى العدل، ومساعدة من ما يقدر يساعد نفسه، ومحاربة الشر**. لكن القوى وحدها ما تكفي — لازم تكون عند الشخصية **الرغبة بفعل الصح**.

أفلاطون كان يعتقد إن **الخير جذّاب بطبعه** — يسحبنا نحوه طالما ما محجوب عن نظرنا. لهذا الأبطال الخارقين يُصوَّرون كقوى أخلاقية. من الطفولة للبلوغ، يذكّروننا بأهمية **ضبط النفس، والتضحية بالذات، واستخدام حياتنا لشي خيّر**. وبقوا محبوبين لأنهم **يخاطبون آمالنا** (نطمح نعمل فرق، نترك أثراً، ونُعترَف بذلك) و**مخاوفنا** (الأذى، المستقبل المجهول).

**سوبرمان** يُلهمنا · **باتمان** يخلّينا نكمل حتى لما الطريق يصير صعب · **سبايدرمان** يورينا نسمع ضميرنا · **ديرديفل** يذكّرنا إن حدودنا ما لازم توقفنا.

### 3. خريطة العناوين الفرعية

| الترتيب | العنوان الفرعي | الفكرة |
|:--:|:--|:--|
| 1 | Defining a Superhero | شنو يخلّي البطل بطلاً |
| 2 | Hope and the Superhero | الأمل والإلهام |
| 3 | Fear and the Superhero | مواجهة الخوف |
| 4 | The Example of the Superhero | القدوة |

### 4. MAIN IDEAS — رتّب الأفكار

| الترتيب | الجملة |
|:--:|:--|
| 1 | **c** — A superhero is a powerful being with abilities beyond ordinary humans and the desire to help others and fight evil. |
| 2 | **e** — A character with superpowers is not necessarily a superhero; the character must also have the desire to do the right thing. |
| 3 | **d** — The image of the superhero is entertaining, but it also inspires us to use our lives for something good. |
| 4 | **a** — By confronting dangers and adversity, superheroes teach us to face our fears and never give up. |
| 5 | **b** — The example of the superhero leads us to develop our own talents and find strength in ourselves. |

**الخدعة:** أول عنوان فرعي ياخذ **جملتين** (c ثم e)، وكل عنوان بعده جملة واحدة.

### 5. DETAILS — أسئلة وأجوبة

| # | السؤال | الجواب |
|:--:|:--|:--|
| 1 | What do superheroes represent and help us deal with? | They **embody our deepest hopes and fears**; they represent our highest ambitions and help us deal with our **worst nightmares**. |
| 2 | Do all superheroes have superpowers? | **No.** Not every character with superpowers is a superhero, and some have no superpowers — they have ordinary human abilities **developed to a superhuman level**. What matters is the **desire to do the right thing**. |
| 3 | What did Plato believe about "good"? | That **good is inherently attractive** — it pulls us in its direction as long as we are not blocked from seeing it. |
| 4 | Why have superhero stories been popular for so long? | Because they **speak to our hopes** — we all aspire to make a difference, have a positive impact, and be acknowledged for it. |
| 5 | What are we inclined to do when we face adversity? | We are inclined to **give up and find an easier path**. |
| 6 | What lesson can Batman teach us? | Batman can **keep us going even when the going is very tough**. |

### 6. مفردات في السياق

| الجملة | الكلمة | المعنى |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Heroes **embody** the best human qualities. | embody | يجسّد |
| They have the **resolve** to keep fighting. | resolve | عزم |
| We **aspire to** make a difference. | aspire to | نطمح إلى |
| We **pursue** our goals. | pursue | نسعى إلى |
| They face **adversity**. | adversity | محنة |
| We **confront** our fears. | confront | نواجه |
| We are **inclined** to give up. | inclined | مائل |
| We are **constrained** by fear. | constrained | مقيّد |

### 7. Relative clauses في النص

| الجملة | الجملة النسبية | الاسم |
|:--|:--|:--|
| A hero is someone **who embodies the best human qualities**. | who embodies... | someone |
| Superheroes face questions **that we will face someday**. | that we will face... | questions |
| Comic books **that tell stories about superheroes** are popular. | that tell stories... | Comic books |
| Superheroes do things **which we would like to be able to do**. | which we would like... | things |

## Reading 2 — Heroes in Real Life

### 1. ملخص عربي — يوهانس

**Yohannes Gebregeorgis** من إثيوبيا. بعمر 19 استعار رواية **Love Kitten** غيّرت حياته. أبوه كان **تاجر ماشية أمّي**، لكن أصرّ إن ولده يتعلم. صار **أمين مكتبة أطفال**، سافر لأمريكا 1981، موّل دراسته، وحصل على شهادة دراسات عليا بعلم المكتبات. اشتغل في مكتبة سان فرانسيسكو للأطفال 1985، وهناك أدرك **أثر كتب الأطفال على الخيال والدهشة**.

اكتشف إن **ما موجود كتب أطفال بالأمهرية** ولا عن إثيوبيا، فكتب **Silly Mamma** = أول كتاب أطفال ثنائي اللغة أمهري-إنكليزي. أسّس **Ethiopia Reads** سنة **1988**. بعام **2002** رجع بـ15,000 كتاب، وفتح **مكتبة شولا للأطفال** ببيته — فغمرها الأطفال واحتاجوا خيمتين. بعدها أسّسوا **مكتبة الحمار المتنقلة** (Donkey Mobile Library). يعتقد إن **القرائية والتعليم** راح يحرّران أرضه.

### 2. ملخص عربي — كارمن

**Carmen Salva** من محافظة **خوخوي (Jujuy)** شمال الأرجنتين. مدرّسة من 20 سنة. قبل عشر سنين، هي وطلابها لاحظوا **قمامة كثيرة برّا المدرسة**، فبدأوا يتطوعون ببرنامج حكومي. البرنامج خبا بعد سنة، فأسّست **Esperanza de Vida** (= الأمل للحياة) سنة **1997**.

كل سبت، هي ومجموعة من 60 إلى 100 طالب ووالد ومدرّس **يخوضون الجبال**، بأيديهم أكياس النفايات ووراءهم اللاما. يجمعون النفايات ويفرزون القابل للتدوير. في سبت عادي يجمعون **~60 كيس نفايات**. اللاما تساعد بحمل الحمل، والحكومة المحلية تساعد بنقله لمركز التجميع.

### 3. MAIN IDEAS — جدول المقارنة

| العنصر | Yohannes | Carmen |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Home country | Ethiopia | Argentina (Jujuy) |
| Job | children's librarian | teacher |
| What made the person interested | reading books like *Peter Pan* → the impact on a child's sense of wonder and vision | students noticed a lot of trash outside the school |
| First thing he/she did | wrote *Silly Mamma*, the first bilingual Amharic-English children's book | began volunteering, collecting and bagging waste |
| Organization / year | **Ethiopia Reads** — 1988 | **Esperanza de Vida** — 1997 |
| Mission | bring children's libraries, books and literacy to Ethiopian children | organize young participants to make streets and environment cleaner |
| Major accomplishment | Shola Children's Library (2002) + Donkey Mobile Library | ~60 bags of trash for recycling on an average Saturday |

### 4. DETAILS — صحّح الجمل (كلها خاطئة)

| # | الجملة الخاطئة | التصحيح |
|:--:|:--|:--|
| 1 | He thinks the books students read **in class** are the "spices of education". | It is the books children read **outside of school** that are "the spices of education". |
| 2 | He wrote *Silly Mamma* because he **did not have enough money to buy books**. | He wrote it because he **could not find any children's books in Amharic / about Ethiopia**. |
| 3 | When the Shola Children's Library opened, **very few** children came. | **Hundreds** of young readers came and overwhelmed the three-room home; two large tents were added. |
| 4 | He believes **television and computers** will free his impoverished land. | He believes **literacy and education** will emancipate his impoverished land. |
| 5 | The Jujuy province is known for **water contamination and trash**. | It is known for its **rich culture and spectacular vistas**. |
| 6 | Her organization's efforts are **limited to the immediate area**. | They have **expanded well beyond** the immediate area; other schools joined. |
| 7 | On Saturdays, the group hikes **around the city**. | The group hikes **into the mountains**. |
| 8 | **The volunteers** help transport the recyclables to the drop-off center. | **The local government** helps transport them. |

### 5. استنتاج — Inference

| # | السؤال | استنتاج |
|:--:|:--|:--|
| 1 | What can you infer about Yohannes's childhood? | He grew up in a poor rural area; his father was illiterate but valued education. |
| 2 | Why did hundreds of children come to the library? | They had no access to children's books; the library was a new opportunity. |
| 3 | Why did Carmen start her own organization? | The government program stopped after one year, but the problem remained. |
| 4 | What does "it's never too early to start caring about the environment" mean? | Children can learn environmental responsibility at any age. |
| 5 | Why does the writer mention the donkey? | To show that the library reaches remote villages that have no roads or cars. |

### 6. مفردات في السياق

| الجملة | الكلمة | المعنى |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Every child should have **access** to books. | access | وصول |
| **Ultimately**, education changes lives. | ultimately | في النهاية |
| She had a **vision** of a library in every village. | vision | رؤية |
| Our **primary** goal is literacy. | primary | رئيسي |
| The school **allocated** money to the project. | allocate | يخصّص |
| The **proceeds** went to the library. | proceeds | عائدات |
| The program was **customized** for rural children. | customize | يخصّص حسب الحاجة |
| They **ventured into** the mountains. | venture | يخوض |
| Water **contamination** is a serious problem. | contamination | تلوّث |
| More than 150 **participants** joined. | participants | مشاركون |
| The group **convenes** every Saturday. | convene | يجتمع |
| We need a new **mentality** about waste. | mentality | عقلية |

## 7. أسئلة امتحانية — Reading

### A) اختر الجواب الصحيح

1. What is the main idea of Reading 1?
   a) Superheroes are stronger than ordinary people.
   b) Heroes are defined by their character and actions, not only by powers.
   c) Comic books are popular with children.
   d) Batman is the best superhero.

2. The word **adversity** in Reading 1 is closest in meaning to:
   a) success  b) difficulty  c) power  d) fame

3. According to Reading 1, Plato believed that good is:
   a) impossible to achieve  b) inherently attractive  c) a superpower  d) a social rule

4. What is the main purpose of Reading 2?
   a) To compare two superheroes.
   b) To show that ordinary people can be heroes.
   c) To explain how to start a library.
   d) To describe the mountains of Argentina.

5. Yohannes wrote *Silly Mamma* because:
   a) he wanted to become famous.
   b) there were no children's books in Amharic about Ethiopia.
   c) he had too much money.
   d) his father asked him to.

6. Carmen's organization was founded in:
   a) 1988  b) 1997  c) 2002  d) 1985

7. The word **convene** in Reading 2 is closest in meaning to:
   a) separate  b) gather  c) travel  d) clean

8. What can you infer about the children who came to the Shola library?
   a) They already had many books.
   b) They were eager to read.
   c) They did not like stories.
   d) They came only for the tents.

9. Which sentence uses a relative clause correctly?
   a) The man which helped me was kind.
   b) The book who I read was good.
   c) The hero who saved the child was brave.
   d) The people which came late missed the bus.

10. The tone of both readings is:
    a) angry  b) admiring  c) humorous  d) doubtful

**إجابات A:** 1-b · 2-b · 3-b · 4-b · 5-b · 6-b · 7-b · 8-b · 9-c · 10-b

### B) أجب بجملة كاملة

1. Why do superhero stories appeal to both our hopes and our fears?
2. What makes a real-life hero different from a superhero?
3. How did Yohannes's father influence him?
4. What does Carmen's group do every Saturday?
5. Why is the Donkey Mobile Library important?

**إجابات B (نماذج):**

1. They show us people who face the same problems we face and give us the power to imagine solving them; at the same time, they help us confront our worst fears.
2. A real-life hero has no superpowers; their power is character, discipline, and persistence, and their impact is real.
3. His father was an illiterate cattle merchant, but he insisted that his son get an education.
4. They hike into the mountains, collect trash, and separate recyclable materials.
5. It brings books to remote villages that have no libraries or roads, so it makes literacy accessible.
