## WRITING HOME — Sophie's email (SB ص 7)

### Ex 4 — Read Sophie's email. What is it about? What do you learn about Sophie's likes and dislikes? Who do you think Catherine is? Ask and answer the questions with a partner.

**فكرة التمرين:** قراءة رسالة Sophie لوالديها من رحلة (نيوزيلاندا/أستراليا) + 9 أسئلة WH- لتكملها وتجاوب عليها مع زميل. (نوعه فهم مقروء — مشاركة بالقاعة.)

**الحل (مختصر، بالإنجليزي):**

| # | السؤال | الجواب |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | How long **has Sophie been** in New Zealand? | Nearly **a week**. |
| 2 | How long **was she** in Australia? | **3 weeks**. |
| 3 | Who does she travel with? | **A friend/travel companion** (they buy a car together = "we"); she **met up with Catherine** in NZ. |
| 4 | Why does she like New Zealand? | **It's smaller and cooler** (than Australia). |
| 5 | Why does she like Kangaroo Island? | **Loads of wildlife** — she saw **platypuses** there. |
| 6 | What is their car like? | **Old** — makes **strange noises**, but going OK so far; bought from "Del's Cars". |
| 7 | Which wildlife **has she already seen**? | **Dolphins, whales, and enormous albatrosses** (plus platypuses on Kangaroo Island). |
| 8 | Where **are they going next**? | **Up the west coast** (of NZ). |
| 9 | Why **is she sending photos**? | **So they won't forget what she looks like** ("I'm sending you some photos so you won't forget what I look like!"). |

**ملاحظة:** "Catherine" = صديقتها/مرافقة السفر اللي التقتها بـ NZ. Sophie ترسل تحياتها ("Catherine sends her best").

#### T1.7 [CD 1: Track 8] — VOCABULARY House and home (الـ 4 حوارات + المركبات) ✅

**الحوار 1:** *(صديقان، واحد يسافر)* — Compounds: **house plants, house-proud**
- A: I'm going away on business for two weeks. Do you think you could possibly water my **house plants** for me?
- B: No problem. I'd be glad to. I'll keep an eye on your whole flat if you like.
- A: That would be great.
- B: Don't worry, I know how **house-proud** you are. I'll make sure everything stays clean and tidy.
- A: I'll do the same for you any time, you know.
- B: Thanks.

**الحوار 2:** *(Julie صارت مديرة UK، ترجع من أمريكا)* — Compounds: **homecoming, housewife, home-made, home-grown**
- A: Julie, have you heard? Anna's just been made managing director of the UK branch of her firm, so she's coming back from the States!
- B: Oh, that's great news. Let's give her a spectacular **homecoming** party when she gets back. Hmmm. She's certainly the career girl of the family.
- A: Doing really well, isn't she?
- B: I know and I'm happy for her. Me? I'm just a **housewife**. Four kids, **home-made** cakes, and **home-grown** vegetables!
- A: And how are my wonderful grandchildren?
- B: They're fine, thanks.

**الحوار 3:** *(حفلة House-warming)* — Compounds: **house-warming, housework**
- A: We're having a **house-warming** party on the 12th. Can you come?
- B: Yes, you bet. We'd love to! But I didn't know you'd moved.
- A: Yeah, two weeks ago. It's much bigger than the old one. Huge kitchen and three big bedrooms.
- B: Sounds great.
- A: Yeah. Mind you, there's much more **housework** to do!
- B: That's a drag!

**الحوار 4:** *(حفلة Carly، جدتها مريضة)* — Compound: **housebound**
- A: Hey, you going to Carly's on Saturday?
- B: I dunno.
- A: It's a free house. It'll be great.
- B: Cool. Where are her parents then?
- A: Carly says they're visiting her grandma — she's sick and **housebound**, so they have to go and help.
- B: OK. Count me in. I'll be there.

#### PRACTICE Ex 3 — Discussing grammar (8 pairs) ✅

1. **Klaus comes from Berlin.** (Present Simple: حقيقة ثابتة — Klaus وُلد في Berlin أو يعيش هناك عادةً.)
   **Klaus is coming from Berlin.** (Present Continuous: جاي الآن بالطريق من Berlin، أو ترتيب مستقبلي.)
2. **You're very kind. Thank you.** (Present Simple: *to be* = state — صفة دائمة.)
   **You're being very kind. What do you want?** (Present Continuous: نشاط مؤقت — *to be* active — يتظاهر باللطف عشان يوصل لشي.)
3. **What were you doing when the accident happened?** (Past Continuous: النشاط اللي كان جاري وقت الحادث.)
   **What did you do when the accident happened?** (Past Simple: الإجراء اللي صار *نتيجة* الحادث.)
4. **I've lived in Singapore for five years.** (Present Perfect: بدأ بالماضي ومازال مستمر.)
   **I lived in Singapore for five years.** (Past Simple: فعل انتهى — عاد عاش هناك زمان وما عاد.)
5. **When we arrived, he tidied the flat.** (Past Simple: اللي صار بعد الوصول.)
   **When we arrived, he'd tidied the flat.** (Past Perfect: اللي صار *قبل* الوصول.)
6. **We'll have dinner at 8.00, shall we?** (Future Simple: اقتراح/نية فورية.)
   **Don't call at 8.00. We'll be having dinner.** (Future Continuous: نشاط مؤقت جار بوقت معين بالمستقبل.)
7. **How much are you paying to have the house painted?** (Present Continuous active: *you* = صاحب البيت — النشاط مؤقت الآن.)
   **How much are you being paid to paint the house?** (Present Continuous passive: *you* = الرسّام — نفس الشي passive.)
8. **How do you do?** (Present Simple: تحية رسمية بعد التعريف.)
   **How are you doing?** (Present Continuous: تحية ودية لصديق.)
