### 2C. PRACTICE Ex 3 — 8 أزواج (فرق المعنى / active↔passive) — ص 8
1. *Klaus comes from Berlin* (حقيقة ثابتة) ↔ *Klaus is coming from Berlin* (جاي بالطريق).
2. *You're very kind* (صفة دائمة) ↔ *You're being very kind* (يتظاهر باللطف).
3. *What were you doing when the accident happened?* (نشاط جاري) ↔ *What did you do when…?* (الإجراء الناتج).
4. *I've lived in Singapore for five years* (مستمر) ↔ *I lived…* (انتهى).
5. *When we arrived, he tidied the flat* (بعد الوصول) ↔ *…he'd tidied…* (قبل الوصول).
6. *We'll have dinner at 8* (اقتراح فوري) ↔ *We'll be having dinner* (نشاط جاري بوقت معين).
7. *How much are you paying…* (صاحب البيت) ↔ *How much are you being paid…* (الرسام — passive).
8. *How do you do?* (تحية رسمية) ↔ *How are you doing?* (تحية ودية).

### 2D. LANGUAGE FOCUS — التنسات (ص 7)
- **Max's letter:** ① Present Perfect Simple ② Present Continuous (temporary) ③ Present Simple/Perfect/Past Simple ④ Past Simple (*did*) ⑤ Past Simple ⑥ Present Continuous (future arrangement) ⑦ Present Simple (state).
- **Sophie's email:** ① Present Perfect Simple ② Past Simple ③ Present Continuous (temporary) ④ Present Simple ⑤ Past Simple ⑥ Present Simple ⑦ Present Perfect (present results) ⑧ Present Continuous (future) ⑨ Present Continuous (now).
- **القاعدة:** state verbs (want, like, know…) ما تاخذ continuous. الـ Present Perfect = for/since. الـ Present Continuous = future arrangement.
