## READING 1 — "We All Need a Hero"

### 1) Vocabulary — Matching (SB ص 23–24 · Focused ص 4–5)

**ملاحظات مهمة (الكلمات اللي تلخبط):**
- **inherently** ← *inherent* (متأصل). من النص: *"good is inherently attractive"* = الخير جذّاب بطبعه.
- **constrained** يُستخدم غالباً Passive: *be constrained by* = مقيد بـ.
- **acknowledged for** (مع حرف الجر *for*) = معترف بك على شي عملته.
- **aspire to** مقابل **pursue**: الأول رغبة/توق داخلي، الثاني سعي وعمل فعلي على المدى الطويل.
- **embody** = يجسّد (النص: *"superheroes embody our hopes"* = الأبطال يجسّدون آمالنا).

### 2) MAIN IDEAS — Order (SB ص 27 · Focused ص 8)
> **الخدعة:** استخدم **العناوين الفرعية (subheadings)** كخريطة طريق — كل جملة تخص عنوان بالترتيب.

| الترتيب | الرمز | الجملة | العنوان الفرعي |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | **c** | A superhero is a powerful being with abilities beyond ordinary humans and the desire to help others and fight evil. | Defining a Superhero (بداية التعريف) |
| 2 | **e** | A character with superpowers is not necessarily a superhero — must also have the desire to do right. | Defining a Superhero (نهاية نفس القسم) |
| 3 | **d** | The image of the superhero is entertaining, but it also inspires us to use our lives for something good. | Hope and the Superhero |
| 4 | **a** | By confronting dangers and adversity, superheroes teach us to face our fears and never give up. | Fear and the Superhero |
| 5 | **b** | The example of the superhero leads us to develop our own talents and find strength in ourselves. | The Example of the Superhero |

**الحل النهائي:** `1→c · 2→e · 3→d · 4→a · 5→b` — الجملة c معطاة مسبقاً برقم 1 بالكتاب (نقطة بداية مؤكدة).

### 3) DETAILS — Questions (ص 27–28)
1. Superheroes **embody our deepest hopes and fears**; they represent our highest ambitions and help us deal with our worst nightmares.
2. **No.** Not all have superpowers — some have ordinary human abilities developed to a superhuman level. The essential part is the "hero" character (noble desire to do right).
3. Plato believed **good is inherently attractive** — it pulls us toward it as long as we are not blocked from seeing/appreciating it.
4. Because they **speak to our hopes** — we all aspire to make a difference, have a positive impact, and be acknowledged for it.
5. We are inclined to **give up and find an easier path**.
6. Batman teaches us to **keep going even when the going is very tough** (perseverance / self-discipline).

> **ملاحظة صفية (من الطالب):** بالقاعة ما يكفي الوقت، فيتم كتابة **رقم الفقرة** اللي تحتوي الجواب بالفراغ (مثل ترقيم MAIN IDEAS)، والدكتور ~99% ما يسأل هالأسئلة فعلياً. ملاحظة: الجواب بكل سؤال موجود بحرفية بالنص — ابحث عن الكلمة المفتاحية (Plato / adversity / Batman...) وراجع فقرتها.

### 6) DETAILS — Correct the FALSE statements (ص 34)
**Yohannes**
1. FALSE → Books read **outside** school are "the spices of education" (not textbooks/in class).
2. FALSE → He wrote *Silly Mamma* because he **could not find** any children's books in Amharic / about Ethiopia (not lack of money).
3. FALSE → When Shola Library opened, children **quickly overwhelmed** the home (needed two extra tents) — not "very few".
4. FALSE → **Literacy and education** will emancipate his land (not TV/computers).

**Carmen**
5. FALSE → Jujuy is known for its **rich culture and spectacular vistas** (contamination/trash is what she fights, not what it's known for).
6. FALSE → Efforts have **expanded well beyond** the immediate area (other schools joined).
7. FALSE → The group hikes **into the mountains** (not around the city).
8. FALSE → The **local government** transports recyclables to the drop-off center (volunteers collect them).

### 8) WRITING SKILL — Paragraph organization (SB ص 38–40 · Focused ص 19–21)
**Activity A** — Analyze the paragraph "Successful people share three common qualities...":
- **Topic sentence:** *"Successful people share three common qualities that allow them to stand out."* — Topic = **Successful people**; Controlling idea = **share three common qualities that allow them to stand out**.
- **Sentence that breaks unity (cross out):** *"They also work longer hours."* (does not develop any of the three qualities).
- **Transition words:** First · Second · For example · Finally.
- **Best concluding sentence:** **(a)** *"Anyone can stand out if they develop their organization, focus, and goals."* (restates the controlling idea).

**Activity B** — Order sentences a–h into a paragraph:
**Order: 1→c · 2→a · 3→d · 4→b · 5→f · 6→g · 7→h · 8→e**
- c = topic sentence · a,d = supporting 1 (+example) · b = supporting 2 · f,g,h = supporting 3 (desire to be more / courage) · e = concluding sentence.

## GRAMMAR — Restrictive relative clauses (ص 40–41)
> **المفهوم:** الجملة النسبية (relative clause) تبدأ بضمير نسبي (who/that/which) وتعطي معلومة ضرورية تحدد الاسم. Restrictive = لا يمكن حذفها (تغيّر المعنى). بتمرين A: تخطّ (underline) الجملة النسبية وتدوّر (circle) الاسم اللي تحدده. القاعدة: الجملة النسبية تبدأ من الضمير النسبي وتنتهي بنهايتها.
**Activity A** (underline clause / circle noun):
1. character [that has superpowers]
2. abilities [which they have developed to a superhuman level]
3. novel [that changed his life forever]
4. merchant [who insisted that his son have an education]
5. children [who have no access to television]
6. group [which is trying to clean up the city]
7. trash [Salva's group collects]

**Activity B** (combine):
1. We all aspire to do something **that** other people will respect.
2. A superhero helps people **who/that** cannot help themselves.
3. Superheroes engage in activities **that/which** we would like to experience.
4. To me, a person **who/that** inspires others to do good deeds is a hero.
5. Reading books for pleasure gives students something **that/which** they cannot get in regular textbooks.
6. Caring for the environment is something **that/which** we can all do.
7. Someone **who/that** donates money to charity is a generous person.

**Activity C** (pronoun can be OMITTED — object relative clauses): **sentences 1, 3, 5, 6**.

## WRITING — Analysis paragraph (SB ص 42–45 · Focused ص 23–26)
> **طبيعة التمرين:** إنتاجي (ما له جواب واحد). الهدف: تكتب فقرة تحليلية تجاوب على سؤال الوحدة *"What makes someone a hero?"* باستخدام مفردات الوحدة + جمل نسبية تقييدية + كلمات انتقال، وبهيكل فقرة صحيح (مقدمة بموضوع + أفكار داعمة متسلسلة + خاتمة).

**PLAN AND WRITE**
- **A. BRAINSTORM:** جدول 3 أشخاص تراهم أبطال → لكل واحد: الصفات (Qualities) + إنجاز مثال (Accomplishments). بعدها قارن: شنو الصفات المشتركة؟ وشلون الإنجازات متشابهة/مختلفة؟
- **B. OUTLINE:** من جدول A سوِّ الـ outline:
  1. **Topic sentence** فيها controlling idea واضحة.
  2–4. **Supporting sentences** (A/B/C/D) منظمة بوحدة وترابط (unity & cohesion).
  5. **Concluding sentence** (E) تلخّص الفكرة.
- **C. WRITE:** اكتب الفقرة من الـ outline + استخدم relative clauses + كلمات الانتقال: *First / Second / For example / For instance / In addition / Also / Finally / Most importantly*.

**REVISE AND EDIT**
- **A. Peer Review:** 4 أسئلة (هل تشرح شنو يخلي واحد بطل؟ شنو الصفات المطوّرة؟ هل الداعم منظم منطقياً وكله يطوّر الموضوع؟ هل توكّف من أفكار الكاتب؟)
- **B. Revise** حسب ملاحظات الزميل.
- **C. SELF-ASSESSMENT checklist (6 عناصر):**
  1. Does the paragraph have a topic sentence with a clear controlling idea?
  2. Are there enough details to support the topic sentence?
  3. Are relative clauses used correctly?
  4. If transition words are included, are they used appropriately?
  5. Does the essay include vocabulary from the unit?
  6. Did you check the essay for punctuation, spelling, and grammar?

**نموذج فقرة (Model — للمراجعة فقط، اكتب عن بطل تعرفه أنت):**
> A person **who** truly deserves to be called a hero is someone **who** puts others before himself. **First**, heroes **confront** adversity with courage rather than giving up when the going is tough. **For example**, Yohannes Gebregeorgis, a children's librarian **who** founded Ethiopia Reads, brought literacy to children **who** had no **access** to books. **In addition**, real heroes **embody** the hope that ordinary people can make a difference. **Most importantly**, they **aspire** to do something **that** other people will respect and **that** leaves a lasting impact. Anyone can become a hero by developing these qualities and using them to help others.

> **ملاحظة:** هذا التمرين إنتاجي — المعلم (د. حيدر) يطلب تسليمه أو نقاشه بالقاعة. النموذج فوق يبيّن الهيكل (Topic → Supporting ×3 → Concluding) مع relative clauses + مفردات الوحدة (confront / adversity / embody / aspire / access). بدّل يوهانس بشخص تعرفه أنت. هذا التمرين **للتسليم/النقاش** (مو مجرد مشاركة) — فهيّئ فقرة جاهزة قبل الحصة.
