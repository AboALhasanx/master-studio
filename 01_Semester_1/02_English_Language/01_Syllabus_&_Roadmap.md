## 1. Course Overview & Academic Competency Matrix

This course elevates the postgraduate student's language capability to the level required for international research publishing, thesis drafting, and formal conference/seminar defense. The curriculum integrates units from *Q: Skills for Success 4 (Reading and Writing)* with rigorous Academic Word List (AWL) vocabulary and scientific grammar conventions.

## 2. Detailed 16-Week Chronological Roadmap

### 📖 Phase 1: Academic Reading, Tenses & Vocabulary Architecture (Weeks 1–4)

- [x] **Week 01: Course Orientation, Diagnostic Evaluation & EAP Foundations**
  - **Topics:** Academic vs. General English distinctions, Diagnostic reading and grammar assessment, Overview of *Q: Skills for Success 4* and Oxford 3000/5000 academic tiers.
  - **Primary Materials:** Course syllabus + Diagnostic worksheet (`02_Raw_Materials/`).
  - **Deliverables:** Personal error log initialized in `LEARNER_MODEL.md`.
  - **Self-Assessment:** Distinguish between informal, professional, and academic registers across 10 sample sentences.

- [x] **Week 02: Unit 1 (Q: Skills 4) — Reading Strategies & The English Tense System**
  - **Topics:** Unit 1 ("No Place Like Home"), Skimming for the main idea, Scanning for numerical/factual evidence, Diagnostic review of English tense structures (Present Simple, Present Continuous, Past Simple, Present Perfect).
  - **Primary Materials:** `Upper Intermediate - Unit 1 - No Place Like Home.pdf` + Student Book Unit 1.
  - **Deliverables:** Completed reading comprehension questions in `03_Study_Notes/`.
  - **Self-Assessment:** Complete "TEST YOUR GRAMMAR" diagnostic exercises on time expressions and verb aspects.

- [ ] **Week 03: Unit 1 Vocabulary & Grammar Consolidation — Collocations & Compound Words**
  - **Topics:** Collocations in academic writing (*conduct an experiment, formulate a hypothesis, pose a threat*), Compound nouns and adjectives in computer science, Time prepositional phrases.
  - **Primary Materials:** `CamScanner Scan - Grammar Worksheet (No Place Like Home).pdf`.
  - **Deliverables:** Collocation bank entry in `03_Study_Notes/Vocabulary_Collocations.md`.
  - **Self-Assessment:** Correct 15 common prepositional and collocation errors in technical sentences.

- [ ] **Week 04: Academic Sentence Architecture — Avoiding Run-ons, Comma Splices & Fragments**
  - **Topics:** Independent vs. Dependent clauses, Coordinating vs. Subordinating conjunctions, Conjunctive adverbs (*furthermore, consequently, nevertheless*), Semicolon usage in academic prose.
  - **Primary Materials:** Academic writing grammar reference sheet.
  - **Deliverables:** Sentence transformation worksheet in `03_Study_Notes/`.
  - **Self-Assessment:** Rewrite 10 run-on sentences extracted from computer science draft abstracts into elegant, compound-complex sentences.

---

### ✍️ Phase 2: Critical Analysis, Academic Word List & Summary Writing (Weeks 5–8)

- [ ] **Week 05: Unit 2 (Q: Skills 4) — Critical Reading & Distinguishing Fact from Interpretation**
  - **Topics:** Identifying author bias, Distinguishing empirical facts from theoretical interpretation, Contextual vocabulary inference, Reading dense technical abstracts.
  - **Primary Materials:** *Q: Skills 4* Unit 2 ("Behavior and Culture").
  - **Deliverables:** Critical reading analysis notes in `03_Study_Notes/`.
  - **Self-Assessment:** Annotate a 300-word excerpt identifying the claim, supporting evidence, and underlying assumptions.

- [ ] **Week 06: Academic Word List (AWL) Sublists 1–3 & Academic Register Transformations**
  - **Topics:** Mastering 180 high-frequency academic word families (e.g., *analyze, establish, indicate, require, significant, approach, concept*), Morphological shifts (noun $\rightarrow$ verb $\rightarrow$ adjective), Eliminating informal phrasal verbs (*look into $\rightarrow$ investigate, find out $\rightarrow$ ascertain*).
  - **Primary Materials:** Coxhead's AWL Sublists 1–3 reference guide.
  - **Deliverables:** AWL Flashcard deck export in `07_Quizzes_&_Anki/`.
  - **Self-Assessment:** Convert a 150-word informal paragraph into formal academic English using $\ge 8$ AWL terms.

- [ ] **Week 07: Paraphrasing, Summarizing & Ethical Citation Discourse**
  - **Topics:** Four-step paraphrasing technique (Change structure + Replace with synonyms + Retain technical terms + Verify meaning), Synthesizing multiple authors, Citation integration verbs (*argues, contends, demonstrates, notes*), Plagiarism avoidance.
  - **Primary Materials:** IEEE Citation and Paraphrasing Guidelines.
  - **Deliverables:** 3-paragraph literature synthesis exercise in `03_Study_Notes/`.
  - **Self-Assessment:** Paraphrase 3 complex paragraphs from an IEEE software architecture paper without triggering similarity overlap.

- [ ] **Week 08: Midterm Examination & Language Mastery Evaluation**
  - **Exam Focus:** Reading comprehension, AWL vocabulary in context, grammatical sentence correction, and academic paragraph drafting.
  - **Weight:** 30% of coursework grade.
  - **Deliverables:** Midterm exam performance reflection and error correction in `LEARNER_MODEL.md`.

---

### 🔬 Phase 3: Research Paper Anatomy & Scientific Voice (Weeks 9–12)

- [ ] **Week 09: Unit 3 (Q: Skills 4) — Cause-Effect Discourse & Logical Connectors**
  - **Topics:** Cause-effect relationship signaling (*as a consequence of, attributed to, precipitated by*), Problem-solution paragraph structures, Reading complex technological impact studies.
  - **Primary Materials:** *Q: Skills 4* Unit 3.
  - **Deliverables:** Cause-effect analytical essay notes in `03_Study_Notes/`.
  - **Self-Assessment:** Write a 200-word paragraph detailing the performance impact of database indexing using cause-effect transitions.

- [ ] **Week 10: Technical Research Paper Anatomy — The IMRAD Framework & Abstract Writing**
  - **Topics:** Anatomy of an IEEE/ACM paper: Introduction, Methodology, Results, and Discussion (IMRAD). Crafting the 4-part structured abstract (Background/Problem, Proposed Method, Key Experimental Results, Significance).
  - **Primary Materials:** IEEE Author Guidelines for Transactions.
  - **Deliverables:** Draft 150-word formal abstract for a candidate thesis topic in `03_Study_Notes/Sample_Thesis_Abstract.md`.
  - **Self-Assessment:** Evaluate abstract against the 4 standard structural criteria using peer-review checklist.

- [ ] **Week 11: Hedging, Cautious Language & Stance in Scientific Claims**
  - **Topics:** Epistemic modality (*may, could, would*), Probability adverbs (*presumably, substantially, plausibly*), Approximators (*approximately, roughly, in the order of*), Expressing scientific caution without weakening arguments.
  - **Primary Materials:** Hyland's Hedging in Academic Research guide.
  - **Deliverables:** Hedging transformation drill in `03_Study_Notes/`.
  - **Self-Assessment:** Transform 10 over-generalized, dogmatic claims into appropriately hedged scientific deductions.

- [ ] **Week 12: Data Commentary & Describing Visuals (Charts, Graphs & Architectures)**
  - **Topics:** Vocabulary for upward/downward trends, plateauing, fluctuations (*surge, plummet, level off, fluctuate*), Describing architectural block diagrams and workflows, Writing figure captions and table descriptions.
  - **Primary Materials:** Academic Data Commentary Handbook.
  - **Deliverables:** 150-word data commentary describing a performance benchmark graph in `03_Study_Notes/`.
  - **Self-Assessment:** Write a complete textual analysis of a latency-vs-throughput benchmark figure.

---

### 🎙️ Phase 4: Oral Defense Discourse & Thesis Gateway (Weeks 13–16)

- [ ] **Week 13: Oral Presentation Strategies & Academic Signposting Discourse**
  - **Topics:** Signposting language for academic talks (*"To begin with...", "Turning our focus to...", "This graph illustrates...", "To summarize the core contribution..."*), Pace, vocal modulation, avoiding reading text verbatim from slides.
  - **Primary Materials:** Marp Seminar presentation guidelines (`00_STUDIO_HUB/templates/template-marp-seminar.md`).
  - **Deliverables:** Presentation speech script in `05_Seminars_&_Slides/`.
  - **Self-Assessment:** Record a 5-minute timed presentation walkthrough using standard signposting phrases.

- [ ] **Week 14: Thesis Defense Phrasing & Responding to Committee Interrogation**
  - **Topics:** Diplomatic discourse for handling tough questions from the 3-member committee (*اللجنة الثلاثية*), Acknowledging limitations gracefully (*"While that is an important consideration, within the scope of this study..."*), Clarifying misunderstandings (*"Allow me to clarify our definition of..."*).
  - **Primary Materials:** `03_Thesis_&_Research_Transition/00_Committee_Guidelines.md`.
  - **Deliverables:** Committee Q&A defense phrase cheat sheet in `03_Study_Notes/Committee_Defense_Discourse.md`.
  - **Self-Assessment:** Formulate responses to 5 aggressive hypothetical questions challenging thesis methodology.

- [ ] **Week 15: Mock Oral Seminar Presentations & Peer Review**
  - **Activities:** In-class delivery of a 7-minute academic seminar presentation with Marp slides; peer review and instructor feedback on pronunciation, fluency, and discourse management.
  - **Deliverables:** Evaluated presentation scorecard and recorded feedback notes.
  - **Self-Assessment:** Review instructor feedback and log pronunciation targets in `LEARNER_MODEL.md`.

- [ ] **Week 16: Comprehensive Final Written & Oral Examination**
  - **Activities:** Final examination covering reading comprehension, AWL vocabulary, grammatical precision, abstract writing, and a short oral defense interview.
  - **Deliverables:** Final course score logged in `GPA_TRACKER.md`.
  - **Final Target:** Score $\ge 90\%$ (Distinction / *امتياز*).
