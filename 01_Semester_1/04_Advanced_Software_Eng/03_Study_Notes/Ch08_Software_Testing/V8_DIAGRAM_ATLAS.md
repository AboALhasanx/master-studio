## Extraction and custom-diagram decision

- Fixed models and examples: extract/crop the clearest original from the canonical deck or the scanned book after page mapping. Do not recreate a generic diagram from memory.
- New exercise inputs: derive the exercise-specific test set, decision table, cause-effect graph, flow graph, or DD paths as needed. Such a derived solution is a new answer diagram, not an extracted source figure.
- The original source figures are not independent between the two deck PDFs. A scan image may provide a better copy, but that comparison is still open.
- Dense low-resolution raster figures cannot gain detail from a larger page render. If their labels are unreadable at print size, state that limitation rather than tracing ambiguous edges.
