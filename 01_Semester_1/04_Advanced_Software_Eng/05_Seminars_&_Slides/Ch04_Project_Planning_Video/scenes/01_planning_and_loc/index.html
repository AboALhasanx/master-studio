Software Project Planning & Sizing

The Foundation of Estimation: Scope, Cost, and Schedule

1. Scope Core Driver
  • 🎯 Functional requirements
  • 📐 Software deliverables & size
  • 🔍 System complexity bounds
2. Cost (Effort) Resource
  • 💰 Person-Months (PM) needed
  • 👥 Staffing & engineering team
  • 🖥️ Infrastructure & tooling budget
3. Schedule Timeline
  • ⏱️ Delivery calendar milestones
  • 📅 Calendar months to deployment
  • 🚨 Strict contractual deadlines
💡
Fundamental Law of Software Estimation
You cannot reliably estimate Cost (Effort) or Schedule without first sizing the Software Scope.
لا يمكن تقدير التكلفة أو الجدول الزمني بدقة دون تحديد وقياس حجم نطاق البرمجيات أولاً.

Conte's Formal LOC Standard

Academic standardization and the remaining estimation vacuum

Formal Standard Conte et al. (1986) Definition of Lines of Code
"A line of code is any line of program text that is not a comment or blank line, regardless of the number of statements or fragments of statements on that line."
Included in Conte's LOC:
Executable lines, data declarations, header inclusions, compiler directives.
Strictly Excluded from Conte's LOC:
Blank whitespace lines, stand-alone comments, multi-line comment blocks.
🎯
Pedagogical Verdict
While Conte standardized LOC counting, LOC remains fundamentally unavailable during project inception. Software engineering urgently needed an SRS-level metric.
رغم أن تعريف كونتي وحّد معايير عد الأسطر، إلا أن الكود يظل غير موجود في بداية المشروع، مما أوجب ابتكار مقياس يعتمد على المتطلبات الوظيفية.
Software project planning begins with balancing the classic Triple Constraint: Scope, Cost, and Schedule.
يبدأ تخطيط المشروع البرمجي بموازنة القيد الثلاثي الكلاسيكي: النطاق، والتكلفة، والجدول الزمني.
