Elementary processes categorized by complexity matrices (Low, Average, High)

EQ · External Inquiry Query

Immediate request-retrieval pair. Reads data directly without executing derived mathematical formulas or modifying ILF.

استعلام فوري وقراءة مباشرة دون أي عمليات حسابية أو تعديل.
Low 3
Average 4
High 6
⚡
Weight Hierarchy: Why EO is Weighted Higher than EQ (5 vs 4 Average)
An EO involves computational algorithms, statistical aggregation, or state-trigger logic, whereas an EQ is a simple direct pass-through query.
تتميز EO بوزن أعلى من EQ لأنها تتطلب معالجة وحسابات رياضية مشتقة، بينما يقتصر EQ على القراءة المباشرة.

3. The Diagnostic Test: Differentiating EO vs. EQ

The primary distinction tested in academic examinations and industrial FPA audits

Elementary Feature EI (Input) EO (Output) EQ (Inquiry)
Primary Intent Maintain ILF data Present calculated results Present direct raw data
Mutates Internal ILF? YES (Required) OPTIONAL / NO NEVER (Forbidden)
Contains Derived Formulas? OPTIONAL YES (Required) NEVER (Pure Pass-Through)
Standard Average Weight 4 FP 5 FP 4 FP
🎯
The Golden Exam Diagnostic: "Search Employee" vs. "Calculate Payroll"
Viewing an employee profile by ID is an EQ (direct lookup). Generating a payslip with tax deductions and net salary is an EO (derived math).
عرض بيانات الموظف هو استعلام EQ، بينما توليد كشف الراتب وحساب الخصومات والضرائب هو مخرج EO لوجود عمليات حسابية.
