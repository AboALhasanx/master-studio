Boehm's Constructive Cost Model (COCOMO)

Three Primary Software Project Development Modes

1. Organic Mode Small & Routine

Small, experienced teams working in familiar, in-house environments with relaxed requirements and minimal innovation needed.

Effort = 2.4 × (KLOC)1.05
Tdev = 2.5 × (Effort)0.38
2. Semidetached Medium & Mixed

Intermediate team size with mixed experience levels. Combination of rigid and flexible requirements (e.g., DBMS or new OS utility).

Effort = 3.0 × (KLOC)1.12
Tdev = 2.5 × (Effort)0.35
3. Embedded Mode Complex & Strict

Tight hardware, software, and operational constraints. Strict regulations with high innovation (e.g., Avionics, Medical Devices, Banking Core).

Effort = 3.6 × (KLOC)1.20
Tdev = 2.5 × (Effort)0.32
Key Insight: As project complexity rises from Organic to Embedded, the exponent b increases from 1.05 to 1.20, reflecting superlinear diseconomy of scale.

COCOMO Mathematical Formulation & Equations

Effort (Person-Months) and Development Duration (Months)

1. Effort Equation (Person-Months)
Effort = a × (KLOC)b

• KLOC: Thousands of Delivered Source Lines of Code.
• a, b: Empirical coefficients determined by the mode.
• Non-linear exponent b > 1 represents communication overhead in larger teams.

2. Nominal Time to Develop (Months)
Tdev = c × (Effort)d

• Tdev: Total calendar months required to deliver.
• c, d: Schedule coefficients (c typically 2.5, d from 0.32 to 0.38).
• Sublinear exponent d < 1 demonstrates Brooks' Law: schedule cannot shrink proportionally by adding people.

Average Staff Size Formula: Staff = Effort / Tdev
Calculates the average number of full-time engineers needed over the project lifecycle.

Putnam Model & Rayleigh Manpower Distribution

Dynamic Staffing Distribution Over Project Lifecycle

Putnam's Software Equation
S = Ck × K1/3 × td4/3

• S: Software size in LOC.
• Ck: Technology capability / environment factor.
• K: Total lifecycle effort in Person-Years.
• td: Delivery time in years.

Critical Schedule Tradeoff: Compressing delivery time requires an exponential 4th-power increase in total effort!
