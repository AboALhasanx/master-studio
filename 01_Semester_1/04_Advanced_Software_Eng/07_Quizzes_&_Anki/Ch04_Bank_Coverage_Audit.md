## Student's format decision

Use concise MCQs that ask the student to recognize a formula, interpret what a formula expresses, distinguish model assumptions or stages, or identify a key note. Do not turn the chapter's worked exercises into long problem-and-numeric-result questions. Detailed calculations remain in the printable calculation pack. Keep each bank within the agreed per-bank limit and do not pad with filler.
