## Student format decisions

Use concise, bilingual MCQs that test definitions, formula or model meaning, notation, assumptions, distinctions, and key design choices. Keep scenarios short. Do not turn these into long numerical-result questions. Keep every vocabulary bank within the agreed 8–16 question range and avoid filler.
