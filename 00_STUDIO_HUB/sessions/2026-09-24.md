# Session 2026-09-24

## 1. Academic PDF Engine & Template Typography Upgrades
- **Problem Diagnosed:** The student reported visual defects on newly generated PDFs:
  1. Internal agent scaffolding (`File 01 of 10`, `**EN.**`, `**AR.**`, `[THIN]`, build footers) leaked into titles and text.
  2. ASCII diagram in §4.3 rendered poorly in formal PDFs.
  3. Tables in Source Notes and Page Anchors overflowed and clipped horizontally due to `white-space: nowrap !important` on `td.ltr-cell`.
  4. In bilingual mode, English quotes and paragraphs rendered with blue text and large spacing gaps, with borders placed on the wrong side.
  5. Retrieval Set Q&A pairs used identical formatting to textbook quotes.
  6. Top header banner had cluttered two-column metadata (`University of Wasit...`) and a redundant badge.
- **Engine Solutions in `pdf_exporter.py`:**
  1. **Table Text Wrapping:** Removed `nowrap` from `td.ltr-cell`; all textual cells enforce `white-space: normal !important; word-wrap: break-word;`. Pure English tables in RTL notes get `dir="ltr"`.
  2. **Active Recall Q&A Cards (`.qa-card`):** Built `transform_qa_cards()` to intercept question-answer pairs and format them into clean cards (soft mint `#f0fdf4` background, 4px Emerald Green border `#10b981`, navy question number badge). Completely distinct from royal blue textbook citations.
  3. **Strict Language-Sensitive Borders:** CSS rules enforce border placement by language direction: on the **LEFT** for English citations/answers, and on the **RIGHT** for Arabic, regardless of document-level RTL/LTR.
  4. **Centered 3-Tier Header:** Refactored `.doc-header` into a centered academic layout (`Unit Eyebrow` + `Main Title` + `Thesis Subtitle`). Removed side university metadata and badge chips.
  5. **Automated Markdown Sanitization:** `sanitize_markdown_for_publication()` automatically purges `File 01 of 10`, `**EN.**`, `**AR.**`, `[THIN]`, and build footers before HTML compilation, and strips leading `# Title` from the body to prevent duplicate headers.
  6. **Iterative File Lock Resolver:** Catches Windows `PermissionError` across multiple open viewers, automatically finding the next available candidate (`_new.pdf`, `_v2.pdf`, `_v3.pdf`).
  7. **High-Res Vector Diagram:** Built 2x retina graphic `06_Diagrams_&_Mindmaps/sdlc_process_methodology_hierarchy.png` replacing the ASCII code block.

  - `04_Advanced_Software_Eng/08_PDF_Exports/Week_02_File_01_SDLC_Fundamentals_Bilingual_v3.pdf`

## 3. WorkBuddy Unit Notes Audit & Comprehensive Fix (Units 02–05)
- **Issues Reported by Student:**
  1. `u5` (RAD) page 9: Q/A formatting failed to render as cards.
  2. `u4` (Incremental) page 7 & 11: Fake `Q` boxes appeared in the middle of lecture body; quotes had broken fonts and distorted bold/italic text.
  3. `u3` (Prototyping) page 6 & 12: Fake `Q` boxes appeared in lecture content; distorted asterisks.
  4. `u2` (Waterfall) page 16: Fake `Q` box appeared over comparison section.
- **Root Causes Identified & Solved:**
  1. **Unscoped QA Matching:** `transform_qa_cards()` previously ran globally across the whole HTML document with `re.DOTALL`, which caused ordinary numbered lists in the lecture body (e.g. `1. تقليل الأخطاء...`) to falsely match a distant blockquote and become fake Q cards.
     - **Fix:** `transform_qa_cards()` now strictly partitions the document at the `## Retrieval set` heading. The entire lecture body before the retrieval set is 100% immune from QA transformation.
     - **Unified QA Patterns:** Extended parser inside retrieval sets to match `[RS-XX-YY]` patterns and strip redundant `Answer:` prefixes.
  2. **Unbalanced Markdown Asterisks:** WorkBuddy wrote `***Heading.**` or `***Text.*"` inside blockquotes, leaving unclosed bold tags that leaked and turned entire subsequent paragraphs bold.
     - **Fix:** Added regex sanitizer in `pdf_exporter.py` repairing `***` -> `**`, balancing quote asterisks, and purged all broken asterisks from `u2`, `u3`, `u4`, and `u5` on disk.
- **Verified Deliverables (Visually Inspected with PyMuPDF):**
  - `u5_fixed_p9.png`: All 16 RAD questions render as pristine emerald cards with `RS-05-01` navy badges and clean answers.
  - `u4_fixed_p7.png` & `p11.png`: Fake Q boxes 100% eliminated; Sommerville quotes and subsequent paragraphs render with crisp, normal body fonts.
  - `u3_fixed_p6.png` & `p12.png`: Fake Q boxes 100% eliminated; vector comparison diagram and tables render flawlessly.
  - `u2_fixed_p16.png`: Fake Q box 100% eliminated; Waterfall vs. Iterative comparison diagram and table render cleanly.
