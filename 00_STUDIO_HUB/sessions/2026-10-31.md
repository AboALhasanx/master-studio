## Typography + real master chrome (same day, second revision)

Student feedback: the figure caption/source/takeaway were still too small, and the
header/footer were per-slide textboxes — not a template.

**Typography research recorded** in
`90_Shared_Toolbox/sandbox/academic_deck_lab/skill/TYPOGRAPHY_STANDARDS.md`:
Microsoft 18 pt floor; Harvard 18–24 pt body; ARL 24 pt; Kawasaki 30 pt;
6×6 density; ISO 9241-306 20′–22′ comfortable / 16′ minimum; ANSI/HFES 100
16′ minimum, 22′–30′ should; WCAG 4.5:1 normal / 3:1 large; Material 3 body
16 px; Apple HIG body 17 pt.

**Measured at a 3 m screen / 6 m distance:** 8.2 pt = 14.7′ (failed the 16′
minimum), 10.5 pt = 18.8′ (below comfortable), 11.5 pt = 20.6′ (comfortable),
14.5 pt = 26.0′, 18 pt = 32.2′, 24 pt = 43.0′.

**Applied:** body 24 pt, title 28 pt, table 15–16 pt, chart 12–13 pt, caption
14.5 pt, source 11.5 pt, header/footer 11 pt, kicker 11 pt; minimum font 11 pt,
0 slides below 10 pt. The figure slide is now image + right rail, so the caption
has room to breathe; source credit is 11.5 pt.

**Real header/footer:** moved to master-level shapes (`Master Header`,
`Master Footer`, `Master Slide Number`), with `p:hf ftr="1" sldNum="1"` and an
`a:fld type="slidenum"` field. Cover/section slides use `showMasterSp="0"` to hide
the chrome. No per-slide footer textboxes remain. Verified in PowerPoint: the
number updates 1/2/3 across slides and disappears on the hidden-chrome slide.

**Status:** 32 slides, 12 visual families, 0 hard QA failures, 31 smooth fades +

## Cosine Morph pair (same day, seventh revision)

Student asked for a real explanatory Morph, not just fades. The scikit-learn
comparison image plan was dropped by request.

**Built:** slides 9–10, a native-vector Morph pair.
- Slide 9: A and B point the same way; `cos = 1`, `θ = 0°`.
- Slide 10: B rotates to 90°; `cos = 0`, `θ = 90°`.
- Shared names: `!!vecA`, `!!vecB`, `!!origin`, `!!labelA`, `!!labelB`,
  `!!angleLabel`, `!!cosLabel`, `!!circle`, `!!formula`, `!!card_left`,
  `!!card_formula`.
- Only B rotates; the formula, circle, and A are static. This is the
  explanatory-Morph rule: the state change is the lesson.
- Morph duration 0.9 s; slide 10 carries the Morph transition.
