### 2. Core Curriculum & Conceptual Breakdown
- **The 5 Core Principles of Swarm Intelligence (Millonas 1994):**
  1. *Proximity Principle:* Population performs simple spatial and temporal computations relative to neighbors (no centralized coordinator).
  2. *Quality Principle:* Population responds to environmental quality cues (fitness function, food/nectar concentration).
  3. *Diverse Response Principle:* Activities not committed along excessively narrow channels; maintains exploration breadth to prevent premature convergence.
  4. *Stability Principle:* System mode of behavior remains steady against minor environmental fluctuations and noise.
  5. *Adaptability Principle:* Behavioral shifts triggered when justified by the computational cost (e.g., when current trajectory fails to reach target).
- **Algorithms Covered:**
  - **Ant Colony Optimization (ACO):** Blind navigation, pheromone deposition and evaporation ($\rho$), double-bridge experiment (8-minute convergence to shorter path), and transition probability formula:
    $$p_{ij}^k(t) = \frac{[\tau_{ij}(t)]^\alpha \cdot [\eta_{ij}]^\beta}{\sum_{l \in N_i^k} [\tau_{il}(t)]^\alpha \cdot [\eta_{il}]^\beta}$$
    Variants: Ant System (AS), Elitist Ant System (EAS), Rank-Based AS, Max-Min AS (MMAS), and AntNet for routing.
  - **Artificial Bee Colony (ABC):** Tripartite colony roles: Employed bees (exploit known nectar), Onlooker bees (waggle-dance selection), and Scout bees (random abandonment exploration).
  - **Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO):** Position ($X$) and Velocity ($V$) dynamics driven by inertia $w$, personal best ($P_{best}$ - cognitive), and swarm best ($G_{best}$ - social). Presentation was interrupted near minute 28 due to laptop power shutdown prior to numerical walkthrough.
  - **Cuckoo Search:** Brood parasitism mapping (nests = solutions, eggs = new solutions, broken eggs = discarded poor solutions, hatched eggs = surviving high-fitness solutions).
  - **Meerkat:** Division of labor with sentinels providing danger warning alerts.
