## OpenCode — Cross-device sync: two real defects fixed, 27/27 on tablet + computer (2026-10-22)

- **Newest *change* now wins, not newest sync.** `collectBundle()` stamped the bundle with `Date.now()`, so a device that had been offline for days always looked "newer" than the server and would overwrite fresher data from the other device. The bundle now carries `portal_data_touched_v1`, a timestamp bumped only when the data really changes (a finished attempt, a bookmark, a best score, a history clear or a backup restore), and download/upload keep it in step with the server's write time. A device with **no** data but a newer touch can also push the empty state, so a deliberate clear is no longer resurrected by the other device.
- **Switching accounts used to inherit the previous account's data.** After logging out and signing in as a different account on the same device, the old history/bookmarks stayed and were uploaded to the new account's row. The device now records whose data it holds (`portal_data_owner_v1`, taken at login, at device-link adopt, and on boot for accounts that predate the key); a login with a *different* id clears the local personal data first, and the login flow re-reads the local copy and re-renders the catalogue, saved and stats after the pull. A device that has never held an account keeps its anonymous data and uploads it on first login.

## OpenCode — Smart merge for two devices (v99/v100), 17/17 cross-device (2026-10-22)

- **Sync is no longer "the newest bundle replaces the other".** `pullOrPush` now merges the local and the server copies: bookmarks are a **union** (per key the newer `savedAt` wins), the best score per quiz keeps the **higher percentage**, history is a **union** de-duplicated by (time, quiz, score) and capped at 200, and the history's newest `clearedAt` drops anything older. The merged copy is applied locally and uploaded only when it really differs, so the order of the two syncs no longer matters.
