
## OpenCode — Portal bilingual rebuild: schema + bidi + translation pipeline (2026-10-21)

- **Root cause found:** the quiz banks were never bilingual — `question_ar`/`options_ar` were byte-for-byte copies of the English text in all 157 questions, and `explanation_ar` did not exist.
- **Schema (done):** `tools/normalize_banks.py` fills `question_en`/`options_en`/`explanation_en` from the legacy fields and reports gaps (`--check`). All 10 banks normalised.
- **Bidi (done):** `bidiHtml()` in `js/app.js` isolates every Latin/numeric run inside Arabic text with `<bdi dir="ltr">`, applied to stems, options and explanations (live + review). Verified: 41 bdi nodes on a bilingual question; formulas/numbers keep LTR order inside RTL.
- **Pipeline (done):** `tools/apply_translations.py` merges `tools/translations/<bank>.json` (id → question_ar / options_ar[4] / explanation_ar) without touching English.
- **Content (in progress):** first 10 questions of Cyber Ch01 translated (question + 4 options + Arabic explanation). Coverage now: questions 127/157, options 121/157, explanations 10/157.
- **Tests:** new `tests/test_banks.py` — English schema + answer-key integrity (strict), translation coverage printed as a ratchet, and a bidi rendering assertion. Suite now 8 specs.
- **Deployed:** SW v39.
