**HARD-WON VISIO FACTS (reusable):**
1. Visio COM text engine **collapses the space between a Latin word and a following digit**: "Figure 18"→"Figure18", "level 0"→"level0", "1 invoke"→"1invoke". Only fix = newline or reword (put the digit first). Same family as our PDF BiDi bugs.
2. Visio also **reorders a comma that precedes a word/label** on mixed runs ("root module, level zero"→"root module ,level zero"). Avoid commas in Visio text entirely.
3. Connector-label text **auto-rotates along the line**; to keep labels horizontal, draw them as separate untexted rectangles, not as line text.
4. Elbow edges: draw parent-stub + horizontal bus + child drop-stub as three `DrawLine` calls each with `EndArrow` only on the final drop; do NOT draw reverse (child→parent) edges — they cross the whole page.
5. `DrawLine` + `EndArrow=4` is simpler and more reliable than dynamic connectors for hand-laid charts.
6. **Portable lesson for our PDF pipeline:** a standalone **Latin paragraph sandwiched between two RTL blocks** gets flipped by `pdf_exporter` even when fully bold (tried bold, parens, plain — all flipped). The **only** reliable safe forms are: a Latin **heading** (`#### …`), a **table**, or a **blockquote**. Used `#### VISIO VERSION` + a source table → clean. (Add to the RTL pitfalls list.)
7. Visio PNG export is low-res by default (page-size dependent); fine for embedding but not for zoomed digits.
