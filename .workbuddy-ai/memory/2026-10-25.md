## 2026-10-25 (night) — FOUND IT: the full printed book is on Internet Archive, and I built a searchable 240 DPI copy

He pushed me to search communities. The earlier "no digital edition" verdict was **too narrow** — it was true for commercial ebook stores, false for Internet Archive, which I had queried badly (my advancedsearch used `creator:(aggarwal)` and the lending item simply didn't surface).

**Two IA items:**
- `software-engineering-3rd-ed-yogesh-singh-k-k-aggarwal-1-removed` — collection `opensource`, free. 3rd ed., printed **pp.1–307 = Ch1–Ch6**, 327 pp, 233 DPI, Tesseract 5.3.0 OCR. Public files: 132 MB PDF (240 DPI, no text), 17 MB `_text.pdf` (78 DPI, has text), 682 KB `_djvu.txt`.
- `softwareengineer0000agga` — 2nd ed. 2005, ISBN 978-81-241-6381-3, 494 pp, 360 DPI, full OCR, `access-restricted-item: true` → free controlled digital lending.

**What I built:** took the 240 DPI page images from the 132 MB file and the line-level OCR text from `_text.pdf`, then re-emitted every line with `page.insert_text(..., render_mode=3)` (invisible) onto the high-res page. 327 pages in 108 s. Result: 137 MB, 654,101 chars, full-text search verified (cohesion 14 pp, coupling 20, COCOMO 23, Halstead 11, SRS 40, phrase search on "Waterfall Model" works). Only 4 pages with no text (covers/blanks).

**First attempt failed** — inserting word-by-word from `get_text('words')` produced newline-separated fragments and broke phrase search, and long words got truncated. Line-level from `get_text('dict')` → `blocks[].lines[].spans` is the correct granularity. Worth remembering for any future OCR-layer build.

**Placed:** `02_Raw_Materials/Aggarwal_SE_3rdEd_InternetArchive/` (PDF + fulltext.txt, 132 MB, gitignored). Report rewritten: `ASE_BOOK_DIGITAL_HUNT_2026-10-25.md` now says FOUND, with the coverage rule Ch1–Ch6 → IA, Ch7–Ch10 → GitHub chapters.

Community sources mapped too: dpbck.ac.in + CPENTalk.com host the 1094-pp slide deck (identical 7,027,035 bytes); Scribd has 2nd ed 492 pp and 3rd ed 528 pp paywalled; LibGen only 103 pp; Telegram channels are generic book dumps with no index.
