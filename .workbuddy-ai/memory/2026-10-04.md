## What was found (verified against primary sources)

**The grading rules are in Instructions No. 27 of 1982 — NOT No. 26 of 1990.** No. 26 covers establishment/admission/supervision only. Art. 17 of No. 26 keeps No. 27 in force.

- **Art. 24(1)** — grading scale: ممتاز 90–100 · جيد جداً 80–89 · جيد 70–79 · مقبول 60–69 · **راسب 59 and below**. No «متوسط» in postgraduate. **Confirms the 60 per-course floor.**
- **Art. 24(4)** — dismissal if the student fails **more than half** of the *first-semester* subjects in the first attempt (6 subjects ⇒ hard line is 4 failures).
- **Art. 24(5)** — requires «مقبول» in every course **and** a general average of «جيد» (70). On failure of either, re-sit at the **start of the next academic year** in the failed courses **and in courses the student chooses in order to raise the average to «جيد»**. Fail again ⇒ dismissal. **Confirms the 70 average, and that the "re-sit a passed course to raise the average" mechanism exists in the text.**
- **Art. 24(6)** — each course grade is weighted by its credit units.
- **Art. 25** — compensatory courses: «مقبول» minimum, one re-sit only.

**MOHESR announcement 21 Jul 2026** — adopted **60** as the passing threshold in the postgraduate preparatory year (agency-reported only; the underlying letter was not found). Relevant to him: his preparatory year is 2026–2027.

## The answer given to his question 2

There is **no "subject deferral"** in the postgraduate system. The second attempt is a right only for: a real failure (<60), a valid-excuse absence, or an average below 70. So "parking a subject" **is a recorded failure**, producing the «مكمّل» status — survivable (the dismissal line is >half of Semester 1), but it (a) risks dismissal if he fails the re-sit, (b) may not land in a clean holiday (ministry letters treat the year as three rounds: S1, S2, second attempt), (c) does not reduce total work, and (d) consumes his failure margin. Absence is far more dangerous than a low mark — never skip an exam.

The cleaner levers are **«النجاح بالمعدل»** and the annual **«درجات المعالجة»** (curve), which are documented in the ministerial letters above. **Flagged as a fateful decision — the choice is his, not mine.**

## The mark-exchange rate (the correction that matters)

$G = S + 0.7F$, with $S\in[0,30]$ coursework marks and $F\in[0,100]$ final percentage.

$$\frac{\partial G}{\partial S}=1,\qquad \frac{\partial G}{\partial F}=0.7 \;\Longrightarrow\; \textbf{1 coursework mark} = \textbf{1.43 final marks}$$

**So the coursework is NOT "just filling" — it is the cheapest 30 marks in the system.** His own estimate of 18–23/30 is a mistake at the low end: at $S=18$ the final must deliver **74.3%** to reach a 70 course grade; at $S=27$ it only needs **61.4%**.

Required final % for a course grade of 70: S=18 → 74.3 · S=20 → 71.4 · S=23 → 67.1 · **S=25 → 64.3** · **S=27 → 61.4** · S=30 → 57.1.

$$F_{avg} \ge \frac{70 - S}{0.7}$$

| Coursework $S$ (of 30) | Required final average |
|:---:|:---:|
| **0** | **100% — impossible** |
| 10 | 85.7% |
| **15** | **78.6%** |
